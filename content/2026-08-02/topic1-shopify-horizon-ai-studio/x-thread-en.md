**Tweet 1 — Hook**

---

**Tweet 2**
What Horizon AI Studio actually does:

→ Highlight any section on the storefront (hero, product grid, testimonial)
→ Describe the layout in plain language
→ Get a fully-coded section: JSON schema, Liquid markup, CSS — dropped straight into your theme

No page builder. No Liquid knowledge required. The generator is the editor.

---

**Tweet 3**
The A/B testing piece is the real story.

Horizon integrates with Shopify's native split testing framework (expanded in May 2026).

Generated variant → live for a percentage of traffic → automated winner promotion at your confidence threshold.

The design hypothesis is the experiment. You don't have to switch tools to validate it.

---

**Tweet 4**
This matters more than it sounds.

Every page builder before Horizon separated generation from measurement.

You designed somewhere. You tested somewhere else. You manually promoted the winner.

The loop was broken. Horizon closes it: generate → ship → measure → promote. One surface.

---

**Tweet 5**
Honest caveats:

→ No JavaScript-heavy interactions
→ Can't touch Shopify Functions or Checkout Extensibility
→ Doesn't work with app blocks from third-party developers
→ Requires Horizon theme (migrating from Dawn can take 4–8+ hours on customized stores)
→ English-only prompt interface

The constraint architecture is real. The scope is narrow on purpose.

---

**Tweet 6**
What this changes for UX designers working in commerce:

The brief quality becomes the quality ceiling.

How precisely you can describe a layout — the structure, the element exposure, the responsive behavior — determines what comes out.

"A 3-column product feature grid, equal width desktop, stacked mobile, 24px gap, editable CTA per column."

That's a brief that generates well.

**Tweet 7**
The longer shift:

AI-generated layout variants are now conversion-validated.

Design decisions that used to take weeks of back-and-forth — "should the hero be full-width or constrained?", "does the testimonial block go above or below the fold?" — now have a feedback loop measured in hours.

Design intuition meets traffic data. In the same canvas.
