**Post:**

🤖 **What changed in Notion 3.5/3.6:**

Notion just repositioned itself from "shared document tool" to "agent orchestration layer."

Three features, one direction: the workspace is now the context window for every AI tool your team uses.

---

🔗 **Feature 1: External Agents API (alpha)**

You can now chat with, assign work to, and track **Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, and Decagon** inside Notion — just like a human teammate or a Custom Agent.

→ Give them a task directly in Notion
→ Track their progress in the same workspace where your design brief lives
→ See their output without switching tools

The brief and the executor are in the same place. The translation step you used to do manually is gone.

---

📋 **Feature 2: AI Meeting Notes → Custom Agent triggers**

This is the one that changes daily work most immediately.

→ AI Meeting Note finishes
→ Custom Agent runs automatically
→ Tracker updated, Slack recap sent, engineering tickets filed

The meeting is now the trigger. Not someone's reminder to do the follow-up.

The gap between **decision** and **execution** just closed.

---

⚙️ **Feature 3: Notion Workers**

Hosted runtime for custom code. You write the logic, deploy to a secure sandbox — no servers to manage.

The pattern:
- **Custom Agent** = AI reasoning for judgment ("what should happen next?")
- **Worker** = deterministic code for execution ("reliably do the specific thing")

Right split. AI decides. Code acts.

Workers are **free through August 11**, then on Notion credits.

---

🧠 **Why this matters for design teams:**

Your design brief is already in Notion.
Your research notes are already in Notion.
Your meeting decisions are already in Notion.

Now the AI tools you assign tasks to can **read all of it by default** — not something you copy-paste into every new chat window.

The workspace IS the prompt.

---

⚠️ **Honest caveats:**

- External Agents API is alpha — rough edges exist
- Orchestration quality = your Notion structure quality (garbage in, garbage out)
- Workers free period ends August 11

If your Notion workspace is a mess of outdated pages, no amount of agent integration fixes that first.

The infrastructure question just changed.

Before: "Which AI tool should I use for this?"
After: "How well is my workspace structured so agents can actually read it?"

The agents are ready. Is your workspace?
