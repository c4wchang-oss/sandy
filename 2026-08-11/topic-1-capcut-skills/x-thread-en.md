**Tweet 2**
The concept: package any repeatable design workflow into a one-click, reusable capability.

Not a template. Not a preset. A workflow — including prompts, model choices, and editing steps.

You encode judgment once. It runs forever.

---

**Tweet 3**
The example they give: "e-commerce main image set of four, fixed brand-style template."

That's not a design task. That's a creative decision made months ago — now running automatically every time someone needs it.

A Skill is the archived version of your design thinking.

**Tweet 4**
Paired with Seedream 5.0 Pro's layer separation — one click splits a flat image into independently editable layers — the workflow becomes:

• Generate with brand constraints encoded in the Skill
• Edit locally without touching the full composition
• Ship

No regeneration loops.

**Tweet 5**
