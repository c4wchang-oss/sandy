## THREADS (long-form)

"The brief is the URL."

Higgsfield launched Marketing Studio and Supercomputer yesterday. You paste a product page. The system reads it, builds a brand kit from your live website (logo, colors, fonts, tone of voice), and generates a campaign across product shots, ads, UGC videos, marketplace assets, posters, and motion — from 1,500+ templates.

No asset prep. No prompts.

Supercomputer is the layer above it: you describe the assignment in plain language, it builds a multi-step production plan, routes each step to the model best suited for it across OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Kling, and Seedance. Scheduled tasks, shared skills, memory import from Claude and ChatGPT, 30+ app connectors. 100 UGC and ad variants per product in minutes.

The angle I'm tracking: this is the most compressed instance yet of the "reference beats description" pattern. Skisso (yesterday) reads deployed CSS to reduce token count for precision edits. Higgsfield reads a deployed product page to skip brief writing entirely. Same principle, applied at campaign production scale.

The honest problem: Higgsfield auto-generates the brand kit from your live website. But your live website may have already drifted from your actual brand standards — undocumented overrides, launched pages that skipped brand review, regional variants that diverged. The same forensics caveat applies here as with every tool that reads production state: deployed state is a starting point, not ground truth.

If your design system is strong, brand kit auto-extraction is powerful. If it isn't, the campaign will inherit your design debt, consistently, at scale.

The Virality Predictor is worth a watch. It estimates engagement before go-live to help teams triage. Useful for efficiency. Not a substitute for brand judgment or understanding your audience at depth.

The headline shift: AI creative has moved from "generate individual assets" to "run campaigns." The constraint question has changed too — from "how do I prompt for brand?" to "is my live website a reliable brand source?" That's a design systems governance problem, not a prompting problem.
