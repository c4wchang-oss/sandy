## Title
Canva MCP is now self-serve GA — and the per-user auth model is the governance story your team needs to read

---

## Content

Canva's remote MCP server is now open to any developer. No waitlist. Just set up an OAuth app in the Developer Portal and your users can connect their Canva account to any AI tool.

Here's what you can do — and what the per-user auth model actually means for design teams:

**What's now possible from Claude or Cursor:**

1. **Generate designs from a brief** — Describe what you need, Canva builds it. No Canva tab required. Works with existing templates and brand assets already in your account.

2. **Search existing work** — Search your designs, folders, and pages from inside your AI tool. Find what's there before generating something new.

3. **Make targeted edits** — Describe the change, Canva applies it to an existing design. Surgical edits without opening the editor manually.

4. **Export in any format** — PDF, PNG, PPTX, MP4. Directly from your AI workflow without touching Canva.

**For solo designers and small studios:**
This is pure upside. Your account, your AI, your workflow. Minimal friction, maximum flexibility.

**For brand leads and design system owners at larger teams:**
This is the part worth reading carefully.

Per-user only. No org-level authentication. No service account. Every designer connects their own Canva account. The MCP respects their individual plan's permissions — it does not enforce brand template governance across the team.

- If your brand governance lives in process (shared libraries, Notion docs, Slack reminders): per-user MCP access will route around it.
- If your brand governance lives in architecture (controlled template access, locked master files): you're fine.

**Compare with Figma's model:**
Figma MCP = enterprise-managed auth, role-based seats, catalog-controlled access. Governance by architecture.
Canva MCP = per-user OAuth. Governance by individual judgment.

Both are honest, intentional choices. Neither is wrong. They're built for different organizational maturities.

**Senior UX read:**
The pipe from AI tools to Canva's design production is now open. The question is whether your brand constraints were designed for a world where every person on your team has their own AI → Canva production line. If not, design the governance layer now — before the team connects.
