# Threads — Figma Weave on Community (English)
**Format:** Long-form personal reflection, no hashtags, conversational

---

I've been using Figma Weave for a few months now, and I keep hitting the same wall with every AI image tool:

The output is repeatable once, by me, if I remember exactly what I typed.

That's not a system. That's a memory game.

What Figma just shipped — Weave workflows on Community — is a small announcement that points at something much larger. Instead of sharing the image you made, you share the workflow that makes it. Anyone on your team (or anyone on the internet) can open your node chain, paste in their own brand assets, run the same sequence, and get brand-consistent output without knowing what you know.

This feels like the difference between sharing a meal and sharing a recipe.

The node-based structure is what makes it work. Each step in the chain is explicit — an Image Describer breaks down the visual logic of a reference image into text, a Style Combiner blends two sets of visual attributes into a new definition, an Aspect Ratio node automatically resizes to every channel you need. Every step is visible. Every step is adjustable. The process has a shape that another person can read and modify.

Compare that to a prompt. A prompt is a black box. You type, you get. If you get something good, you might save the prompt text — but the context, the iteration, the models you tried and rejected, the tweaks that made it work? Gone. Invisible. Untransferable.

Weave makes that work transferable.

For me, the most interesting case is brand imagery at scale. Every team I've worked on has had some version of this problem: a handful of people know how to generate on-brand visuals, and everyone else either does it badly or waits. A Weave workflow on Community flips that — the knowledge is in the system, not the person.

Honest caveat: the tool is still early. The Figma integration (where you can paste Figma frames directly into Weave as live nodes) isn't fully here yet. The UI takes some time to learn. Not every designer will immediately see where to start.

But the direction is right. AI creativity needs infrastructure — version control, sharing, repeatability. Weave on Community is the first real step toward that.
