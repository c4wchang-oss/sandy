**Tweet 2**
Quick explainer on what Figma Weave actually is:

It's a node-based canvas. You chain AI models together in sequence.

Image describer → style combiner → variation generator → aspect ratio resizer.

Each step is visible, adjustable, repeatable. The process has a shape — not just a result.

---

**Tweet 3**
What "Weave on Community" actually means in practice:

A designer builds a brand photography workflow — virtual try-on with their models, editorial preset, specific aspect ratios.

They publish it. You open it, swap in your brand assets, run it.

You didn't write a single prompt from scratch. You ran their system.

**Tweet 4**
This inverts the "single prompt" problem.

When you prompt AI directly, the creative process is opaque.
You get an output but no recipe.

Weave flips this — the recipe IS the asset.
You share how you made the image, not just the image itself.

**Tweet 5**
