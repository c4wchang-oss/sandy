# Threads Post — Open Design 0.16.0: From Generation to Delivery
**Platform:** Threads | **Date:** 2026-07-25 | **Language:** English

---

There's a pattern in how software matures: the first version impresses you with what it can do. The second version makes what it can do actually usable. The third version makes it trustworthy.

Open Design 0.16.0, released this week, is called "Reliable Delivery." That codename is doing a lot of work.

---

The headline feature: visual direction now follows the artifact you're actually making.

Documents, posters, videos, wireframes, mobile work — each gets style previews calibrated to its output format. Four choices inline, full library one click away.

The tool stopped assuming everything was a prototype and started asking: what are you actually trying to ship?

That's a systems design decision disguised as a UX improvement.

---

Deployment now has explicit Preview and Production targets. The Preview URL is distinct from the live production hostname.

This sounds like a QA feature, not a design feature. But it is the design feature — because "generated" → "actually live" is exactly where most AI design workflows collapse.

The gap between those two states is where designers spend 40% of their time. 0.16.0 is attacking that gap.

---

Also in this release: image generator retries on service interruptions, more reliable automatic updates, model picker stays synced with available models.

None of these are "wow" announcements. All of them compound into "I can actually build a reliable workflow on this."

---

Real headline: Open Design 0.16.0 isn't "more features."

It's the moment an AI design tool starts caring more about what happens after generation than about generation itself.

That's the right problem to be working on right now.
