🚀 **This Version Is Not About More Features**

Open Design 0.16.0, codename "Reliable Delivery."

92 PRs. 20 contributors. 5 days.

A maturity milestone for an open-source AI design tool.

---

🔑 **Three Things Worth Paying Attention To**

① **Visual direction now follows the artifact format**
Documents, posters, videos, web clones, wireframes, mobile — each format gets style previews calibrated to its output type. The tool stopped assuming everything was a prototype. It started asking: what are you actually trying to ship?

② **Deployment has explicit Preview vs Production targets**
Preview URL doesn't overwrite the production hostname. Sounds minor. But "generated" → "actually live" is exactly where most AI design workflows fall apart. This distinction being formalized matters.

③ **Reliability improvements across the board**
Auto-retry on image generation failures. More reliable automatic updates. Model picker stays in sync with available models.

---

💡 **What This Means (For Senior Designers)**

The "generation" problem in AI design tooling got solved fast.

The hard problem now is delivery: can what I generated reliably make it through the full workflow and actually ship?

Most designers' pain isn't "AI generates bad things." It's:

**"After generation, 40% of my time goes to managing the gap between generated and shipped."**

0.16.0 is attacking that gap directly.

---

📌 **Why I'm Tracking This**

Open Design is open-source, shipping a new version roughly every 5 days.

The direction is increasingly clear: not competing with Figma on design features, but building reliable infrastructure for the AI generation → delivery pipeline.

The generation problem is commoditized. Delivery is the current battleground.
