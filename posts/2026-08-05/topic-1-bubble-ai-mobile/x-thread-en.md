**Tweet 1 (Hook)**
Bubble just let the AI agent read your screenshots.

That's it. That's the entire UX shift no one is naming correctly.

---

**Tweet 2**
The Bubble AI Agent went full mobile on Aug 4.

It can now build entire native mobile apps: UI, workflows, data, API connections.

But the part worth your attention is this: you can feed it a screenshot of your web frontend and it replicates the design for mobile.

---

**Tweet 3**
Think about what that means for the design workflow.

Your design artifact — the screenshot — is now the highest-fidelity input for a build agent.

Not a Figma spec. Not a prototype. A screenshot.

---

**Tweet 4**
That's not because screenshots are better than specs.

It's because agents are now good enough to interpret dense visual context directly.

The visual IS the brief.

---

**Tweet 5**
The quality ceiling is still your existing component consistency.

The agent reads your reusable components and reuses them across the mobile build.

Your component naming, your shared patterns — they're now AI-legible constraints.

Design system quality = AI output quality. Again.

---

**Tweet 6**
The shared backend is underrated here.

Web and mobile share the same database and same workflows on Bubble. The agent isn't guessing at your data model — it's extending it.

That's architecturally honest. No duplicate infrastructure.

---

**Tweet 7**
Honest caveat: still beta.

Native platform conventions — bottom sheets, gesture nav, native transitions — are still areas where human judgment matters.

The agent will get the structure right before it gets the feel right.

---

**Tweet 8 (CTA/Reframe)**
The reframe:

The screenshot is no longer just documentation. It's now a design brief your build agent can execute.

What you design visually is what gets built. The handoff layer is thinning.
