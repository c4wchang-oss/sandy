**Tweet 1 (Hook)**
An AI just crawled your production site and built the receipt for your design debt.

**Tweet 2**
DesignSync.AI launched Aug 4 (Sun Asterisk).

Enter a URL. It crawls your entire live website and outputs a structured report of your current design system: colors, typography, spacing, component patterns — all extracted from what's actually deployed.

---

**Tweet 3**
This is not a UX audit tool in the traditional sense.

It's more like design system forensics.

It tells you what your design system actually IS in production, not what your Figma says it should be.

---

**Tweet 4**
The gap between those two documents is almost always uncomfortable.

I've seen teams with 6 shades of blue in prod and only 2 in Figma. Typography scales that drifted over 18 months of "quick fixes." Component patterns rebuilt by three different devs who each thought they were following the spec.

---

**Tweet 5**
What DesignSync.AI produces is a baseline.

Sun Asterisk's designers then work with you from that baseline to create unified, AI-ready design assets.

It's a service + tool combo, not a standalone product you buy and self-serve.

---

**Tweet 6**
The downstream reason this matters:

Claude Design, Figma Make, Cursor — AI code agents are only as good as the design system they're working from.

If your production design system is fragmented, every AI output inherits that fragmentation. You can't prompt your way past a broken foundation.

---

**Tweet 7**
Honest take:

Most valuable for teams with significant legacy debt. Greenfield teams building on AI tooling from day one are less likely to have the divergence problem this solves.

---

**Tweet 8 (CTA/Reframe)**
The reframe:

Your production UI has always had a design system embedded in it — even if you never documented one.

The question is no longer "do we have a design system?"

The question is: "does ours match what we think it is?"
