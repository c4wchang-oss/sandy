**Title: Senior UX take | The design file that now travels everywhere**

---

I've been watching the design.md story unfold since September. This week it clicked.

Anthropic integrated Claude Design into ALL of Claude — every conversation, Claude Code, Artifacts tab. The standalone claude.ai/design site closes December 14. This is now just Claude.

---

**What is design.md?**

A plain text Markdown file with your brand's:
- Colors (exact hex values)
- Typography (font families, sizes, weights)
- Component patterns
- Spacing / layout rules

Not a Figma file. Not a PDF. Plain text that every AI tool can read directly.

---

**How it works now:**

① Upload your logo, brand kit, old decks, or codebase
② Claude extracts your design rules, generates design.md
③ That file travels to:
   → Claude Code (via /design-sync command)
   → Gamma, Canva, Google Slides, Adobe (on export)
④ Every AI tool that reads it outputs in your brand language

---

**Why senior designers should care:**

Brand constraint portability = your brand's design decisions traveling across every AI surface you or your team uses.

One file. Everywhere.

Claude also checks its own output against your design system before showing you. You're reviewing the corrected version, not the first draft.

---

**The honest part:**

Quality ceiling = whatever you put in. A brand that's never been formalized → AI that extracts inconsistency.

GIGO still applies.

---

**The arc I'm watching:**

design.md (Vercel, Sep 2026)
→ Auto-generated by Onefold
→ GUI.md for code agents (Graphical)
→ Native in Claude (Oct 2026)

This is becoming the file format for brand portability in AI-native design.
