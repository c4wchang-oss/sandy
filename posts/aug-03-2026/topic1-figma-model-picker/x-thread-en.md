## Topic: Figma Batch AI Image Editing + Model Picker
## Date: Aug 3, 2026

---

**Tweet 1/8**
Figma just added a model picker to image editing.

Not a minor UX tweak. It's a signal that AI model selection is becoming a core design skill — not just for generation, but for production asset work.

---

**Tweet 2/8**
What changed: Edit Image now lets you choose Gemini 2.0 Flash or GPT Image 1.

Different models. Different aesthetic tendencies. You're not just editing an image — you're directing which AI interprets your intent.

---

**Tweet 3/8**
Batch editing got upgraded too — remove backgrounds or enhance resolution for up to 25 images at once.

Repetitive asset work that used to eat a morning now runs while you're in a meeting. The workflow friction was never about skill. It was about time.

---

**Tweet 4/8**
The pattern here is exactly what Framer did with model tiering (Sol/Terra/Luna).

Every major AI design tool is now building a model selection layer. This is the industry's consensus forming: model awareness is creative direction.

---

**Tweet 5/8**
The uncomfortable corollary: if model choice produces meaningfully different outputs, then designers who understand model tendencies will get better results than designers who don't.

That's a skill gap forming in real-time.

---

**Tweet 6/8**
The Figma Draw tools (Pencil, Brush) that landed earlier this year are part of the same thesis.

Figma wants to be the one canvas where your creative work lives start to finish — from rough sketch to production-ready asset to AI-edited image.

---

**Tweet 7/8**
My take on where this goes: the model picker becomes a design system element.

Teams will have a "preferred models by task type" convention. Background removal → X. Style enhancement → Y. Just like you pick font stacks and color tokens, you'll pick model stacks.

---

**Tweet 8/8**
Real headline: it's not "Figma added two image models."

It's that Figma just made AI model awareness part of the image editing workflow for every designer using the tool.

Model literacy stops being optional.
