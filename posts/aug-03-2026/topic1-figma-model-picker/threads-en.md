# Threads Post — EN
## Topic: Figma Batch AI Image Editing + Model Picker
## Date: Aug 3, 2026

---

Figma just put a model picker in the image editing menu.

You can now choose between Gemini 2.0 Flash and GPT Image 1 when editing images in your design file. Same task. Different creative interpreters.

This matters more than it looks. Every major AI design tool is now building a model selection layer — Framer did it with Sol/Terra/Luna for canvas work, now Figma is doing it for image editing. The industry is converging on a thesis: which model you pick is a form of creative direction.

The batch editing upgrade (25 images at once for background removal or resolution enhancement) is the efficiency story. The model picker is the skills story.

If different models produce meaningfully different outputs for the same prompt, then model awareness is a craft differentiator. Designers who understand the aesthetic tendencies of different models will get better results. That's a skill gap forming in real-time.

My prediction: teams will codify model choice into their design system. "Use Gemini 2.0 Flash for product photography backgrounds, GPT Image 1 for editorial illustration" will become a house convention — just like you choose font stacks and color tokens.

Real headline: Figma didn't just add two image models. It made model literacy part of the standard design workflow.
