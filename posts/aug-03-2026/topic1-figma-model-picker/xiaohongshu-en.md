**Title:** Figma just gave image editing a model picker

**Body:**

You now choose your AI model when editing images in Figma. This is bigger than it sounds.

**What's new this week:**
① Edit Image: choose Gemini 2.0 Flash or GPT Image 1
② Batch editing: remove backgrounds / enhance resolution on up to 25 images at once
③ Figma Draw: Pencil + Brush tools live in the toolbar

**Why the model picker matters:**
Different models have different aesthetic tendencies. Same prompt, different visual interpretations. Picking the right model for the right task is now a creative direction decision — not a technical one.

**The pattern forming across AI design tools:**
- Framer: Sol/Terra/Luna model tiers for canvas tasks
- Figma: Gemini vs GPT for image editing
- Industry thesis: model awareness = creative direction skill

**What to do with your workflow right now:**
Stop treating all AI image edits the same. Start building a mental model of which AI handles which task better. Test both. Notice the differences. Write it down.

**My prediction:**
In 12 months, design systems will include a model selection convention alongside font stacks and color tokens. Teams who figure this out early will move faster on asset production.

Real headline: Model literacy just became part of the standard design job.
