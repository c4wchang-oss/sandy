# Aug 23 — Topic 2: Dovetail Channels 2.0

**Source:** Dovetail Sun's Out launch, July 15, 2026; reliability updates Aug 17, 2026  
**The news:** Dovetail Channels 2.0 — pulls continuous high-volume feedback from 30+ sources (Gong, Intercom, Salesforce, Qualtrics, support tickets, sales calls, app reviews, surveys), generates revenue-weighted, evidence-backed ideas, and sends full customer context (quotes + CRM data + ARR) directly into Claude Code, Cursor, Linear, or Jira in one click. MCP connectors on Claude, ChatGPT, Figma, Teams/Copilot marketplaces. Two-way sync with Linear/Jira. Digital twins from real data.  
**Core angle:** The research-to-code pipeline just closed. The translation layer — findings decks, insight presentations — got bypassed, not just accelerated. Senior researcher/designer role shifts to context curator: curating the channel inputs, tag taxonomy, and signal thresholds that AI agents build from.

## THREADS (English) — Long-form

```
I've been thinking about Dovetail Channels 2.0 since it launched in July, and I finally have words for what it actually replaced.

Not a tool. A role.

The translator between research and product.

Here's what the feature does: Channels pulls continuous high-volume feedback from 30+ sources — support tickets, sales calls, app reviews, surveys. AI turns that into revenue-weighted, evidence-backed ideas. When an idea is ready to build, one click sends the full context — customer quotes, CRM data, ARR attribution — into Claude Code, Cursor, or a Linear ticket.

The person or agent who picks it up starts with real customer evidence attached, not a one-line summary someone wrote in a handoff doc. The context doesn't need to be translated. It travels with the work.

The question this raises for me: if research evidence now reaches the AI building the feature directly — bypassing the findings deck, bypassing the "here's what users told us" presentation — what is the researcher's job?

My answer: you're the architect of the channel.

What sources go in. What the tag taxonomy covers. What signal threshold is worth acting on. Whether the evidence reaching the agent is actually high quality or just high volume. Those decisions are yours, and they don't get automated.

What does get automated: the translation layer. The synthesis document. The insight presentation.

The honest caveat: this only works if your research ops are already clean. 61+ million classified data points sounds impressive — but garbage in, wrong feature out. Tag consistency, source curation, and evidence quality still determine what the agent builds from.

The frame I keep using: constraint architecture moved up another level. You're not writing the design system anymore, you're not writing the design brief anymore. You're curating the context that AI agents inherit when they decide what to build.

That's a senior role. It just doesn't look like any job description that existed three years ago.
```
