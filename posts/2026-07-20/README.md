## Topic 1 — Pixso AI Smart Design: The Design System IS the Constraint Architecture

**Core angle:** Every AI UI generator has the same problem — it halluccinates components your design system never defined. Pixso AI Smart Design takes a different approach: generation is scoped to your actual component library and design tokens. The AI can only use what's already in your system.

**The real headline:** It's not "AI generates UI from prompts." It's "your component library is now your AI constraint architecture." Teams with real design systems get real output. Teams without one get generic noise.

## Topic 2 — Adobe Firefly Becomes a Multi-Model Creative Switchboard

**Core angle:** Adobe added Gemini Omni Flash to Firefly Boards as a selectable model. The surface-level story is "faster iteration." The structural story is bigger: model selection is becoming a native design workflow feature, not a developer API or a separate tool. The designer holds the switch.

**The real headline:** It's not "Gemini is in Firefly." It's "the creative workspace is becoming a model switchboard — and understanding model characteristics is becoming a form of creative direction." Taste applied to tooling, not just output.
