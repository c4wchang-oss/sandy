## Design Notes

- Keep all text at minimum 28px equivalent for Xiaohongshu readability
- Do not use Framer, Figma, Notion brand colors — use neutral palette to avoid IP issues
- Tool logos in timeline can be replaced with minimal wordmarks or geometric shapes if needed
- The lime green (#A3E635) is the only accent color; use sparingly for hierarchy
- No gradients. Flat. Minimal. Dark mode always.
- Font recommendation: Inter or Syne for headlines, JetBrains Mono or similar for code snippets
