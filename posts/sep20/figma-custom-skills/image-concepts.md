## Image 2 — Xiaohongshu (3:4)

**English version**

Format: 3:4, light editorial background (#F8F6F2 off-white)

Layout — three-stacked sections:

**Top section (30%)**: Convergence timeline graphic
- Four pills in a horizontal row with connecting line:
  SKILL.md → Field Guide → Notion Skills → Figma Custom Skills
- Label above: "Same pattern. Every major tool." in small caps

**Middle section (40%)**: Comparison card — dark card (#1A1A2E)
- Title: "Skill vs Plugin"
- Two columns side by side:
  - SKILL column: brain/adaptive icon, "Context-aware", "Judgment calls", "Style review"
  - PLUGIN column: gear/deterministic icon, "Same in → Same out", "Repeatable logic", "Layer rename"
- Bottom label: "This is a product design decision."

**Bottom section (30%)**: Quality ceiling stack diagram
- Three ascending bars labeled from bottom to top:
  "Session quality" → "Skill quality" → "Team output quality"
- Arrow pointing up with label: "Ceiling moves with your intent"

Typography: Clean sans-serif, black on light background for top/bottom, white on dark for middle card
Corner branding: Small Figma logo + "Custom Skills" wordmark, top-right
