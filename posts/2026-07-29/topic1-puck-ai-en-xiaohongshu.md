**Title:** Puck AI just changed who decides what AI can build in your product

Not another AI design tool. An embeddable AI generation layer.

📦 **What it actually is**
Puck AI is an open-source React component (13k+ GitHub stars) you embed inside your own product. Your users can generate, review, and refine production-ready interfaces without leaving your app.

Two modes:
• Assembly mode — AI picks from your existing components to build pages
• Design mode — AI creates new components within guardrails you define

🏗️ **Why the distinction matters**

Assembly mode: your component library is a hard wall. AI cannot generate anything you haven't already built. No prompt trick gets around it. Scope = library.

Design mode: you write the constraint rules. The AI operates inside the vocabulary you define.

🎯 **What it means for your role as a product designer**

1. Your component decisions are now AI generation policies
2. "Should this component exist?" becomes "Should AI be able to build this?"
3. The design system isn't guidance — it's the boundary of AI creativity
4. Constraint architecture now protects your users, not just your engineers

🔧 **Technical notes worth knowing**
• All outputs are structured JSON — versioned, storable, backend-servable
• Headless API available for programmatic generation at scale
• BYOK (bring your own key) for advanced control
• Works as chat interface or headless generation

💡 **The real shift**

The person who defines the component architecture is now defining the scope of AI-generated experiences for every user in the product.

That's a design responsibility that didn't exist two years ago.
