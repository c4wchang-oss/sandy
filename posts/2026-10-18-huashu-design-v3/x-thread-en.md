**Tweet 1 (Hook)**
Huashu Design just hit 25k GitHub stars and shipped V3.0.

It's an HTML design skill you install into Claude Code. One sentence = clickable prototype, motion export, or infographic. No GUI.

What V3.0 teaches me about where design tooling is actually heading:

---

**Tweet 2**
Most AI design tools bet on the GUI.

Huashu bets on the opposite: make the tool layer disappear. You don't open an app. You tell your coding agent. The skill runs.

Claude Design (GUI) vs Huashu Design (skill). Both are right. They're different bets.

---

**Tweet 3**
V3.0 expands style coverage from 60 to 84 — and every style ships with a specimen image.

The agent sees what "done well" looks like before it starts.

That's not just a style library. That's a quality floor built into the tool.

---

**Tweet 4**
The direction selection got a complete rethink.

Six candidates generated in parallel (3 logic systems: best-designer picks / real-world reference / random rotation). 3 finals chosen for being the strongest + most different from each other + most different from your past work.

Your history prevents repetition.

---

**Tweet 5**
The learning system is the part nobody's talking about.

It tracks what you've made, the palette choices, the style calls — stored locally, never uploaded. Before the next project starts, it reads your history back to you.

Constraint architecture that accumulates over time.

---

**Tweet 6**
Motion design is now real.

Stage + Sprite time-slice model. 60fps interpolation. BGM-scored MP4 export. 8–12 minutes per piece.

Not Framer Motion-level animation. But enough for launch sequences, product explainers, and animated decks you'd otherwise skip making.

---

**Tweet 7**
The honest limitations:
– No Figma-editable output (HTML → screenshot → done)
– No 3D/physics/particle animation
– Blank brand = 60–65 quality

"This is an 80-point skill, not a 100-point product. For people who won't open a design GUI, an 80-point skill beats a 100-point product."

That's the design philosophy right there.

---

**Tweet 8 (CTA)**
The frame I keep coming back to:

When your design tool is a sentence, the quality ceiling isn't the GUI anymore. It's the constraints you've built — style library, brand assets, learning history.

The tool disappears. The craft stays.
