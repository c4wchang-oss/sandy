**Body:**

Huashu Design V3.0 just shipped. 25k stars on GitHub, MIT licensed, runs entirely as a skill inside Claude Code / Cursor / Codex.

Here's what it actually does:

① One sentence → clickable iPhone prototype (10–15 min, real bezel, actually tappable)
② One sentence → motion video export (60fps MP4, BGM-scored, 8–12 min)
③ One sentence → print-quality infographic (PDF / PNG / SVG)
④ 5-dimension expert critique on any design (radar chart + Keep / Fix / Quick Wins)

**What's genuinely new in V3.0:**

① 84 design styles (up from 60) — each with a specimen image so the agent sees the goal before executing
② New direction logic: 6 candidates in 3 parallel systems, 3 finals chosen for being most different from each other AND your past work
③ Learning system — tracks your style history locally, reads it back before next project starts
④ design_lint.py — auto-checks for broken images, contrast failures, console errors

**The honest limitations:**
– Output is HTML, not Figma-editable
– No 3D or physics-based animation
– Blank brand gives you 60–65/100 quality

**Install:** `npx skills add alchaincyf/huashu-design`

**The bigger frame:** When your design tool is a skill inside your coding agent, the quality ceiling shifts from GUI proficiency to how well you've built your constraint infrastructure. Style library, brand assets, learning records — that's where the craft lives now.
