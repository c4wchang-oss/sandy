**Title: Figma measured AI's impact on design: 62/100. Here's what the number doesn't tell you.**

Figma just published their AI Impact Index. 3 years of data, 639 AI-moderated interviews. As a senior UX designer, here's what stood out.

✦ **The number: 62 out of 100**
AI's impact on design in 2026. Nearly double from 2024. We've crossed from "experimental" to "operational infrastructure."

✦ **The most honest finding**
"AI increased iteration speed but introduced quality concerns."
That's the tradeoff — stated plainly, from 47 real practitioners. No hype. Just the signal.

✦ **How they built the research tool**
The most interesting part: they built their analysis dashboard by iterating with Figma's own MCP server. Designed the layout, generated code, refined until it worked. They measured AI's impact using the thing being measured. Methodology as proof-of-concept.

✦ **What 62/100 actually means for your org**
→ AI integration isn't a question anymore — it's a given
→ The gap is now governance: who defines quality? Who owns the baseline?
→ Measurement matters: without a baseline, you can't tell if you're gaining or trading

✦ **The senior designer's actual job right now**
Not using AI faster. Building the measurement systems and governance criteria that determine what "good" looks like with AI in the loop.

✦ **The honest caveat**
This is Figma measuring the impact of their own tools. Directional signal is useful. Absolute numbers — apply your own judgment.

Does your org have an AI impact baseline? If not, what decisions are you making without data?
