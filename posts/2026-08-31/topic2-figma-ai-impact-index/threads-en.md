# Threads Post — Figma AI Impact Index 2026
**Platform:** Threads
**Date:** 2026-08-31
**Topic:** Figma AI Impact Index — 62/100 and the governance question it surfaces
**Source:** https://www.figma.com/blog/measuring-the-impact-of-ai/

---

Figma measured the impact of AI on design. 62 out of 100 this year. Nearly double from 2024.

639 AI-moderated interviews. 3 years of survey data. And the methodology they built to analyze it is as interesting as the number itself.

They ran thematic analysis at scale — 639 conversations, tagged by AI, traceable back to individual participants. One theme: "AI increased iteration speed but introduced quality concerns." 47 people said it. You can read exactly what each one said.

Here's what I can't stop thinking about: they built the research dashboard by iterating with Figma's own MCP server. Designed the layout, generated the code, refined it. The tool they built to measure AI's impact was built with the thing being measured.

At 62/100, this isn't experimental infrastructure anymore. The conversation has moved. Not "should we integrate AI?" — that's settled. The real question is governance: who decides what quality means with AI in the loop? Who owns the measurement baseline? Who designs the process others will use?

For senior designers, this is the signal: the differentiation isn't in using AI faster. It's in building the systems that define what "good" looks like in an AI-augmented design process. Measurement, governance, quality criteria. The unsexy work that determines whether the speed gain is actually a gain.

If your org doesn't have a baseline, you're optimizing blind.
