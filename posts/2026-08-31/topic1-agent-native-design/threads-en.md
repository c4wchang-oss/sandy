# Threads Post — Builder.io Agent Native Design
**Platform:** Threads
**Date:** 2026-08-31
**Topic:** Builder.io Agent Native Design — the artifact is the screen, not a picture of it
**Source:** https://www.builder.io/blog/agent-native-design-figma-alternative

---

The oldest gap in our craft isn't between design and dev.

It's between "here's how it should look" and "here's how it actually works."

Builder.io shipped something this month I keep thinking about: Agent Native Design. Free. MIT-licensed. Open source. No signup wall.

The artifact you produce isn't a Figma frame — it's a running HTML page. Real browser. Real interactions. Same source that exports.

You attach your design system, and it generates from your components and tokens. Not a generic house style. Yours.

Here's what changes at the ideation stage: responsive failures, interaction dead ends, empty states — these usually surface at staging, weeks after decisions were locked. With a live HTML prototype, they surface in week one, before anyone wrote actual production code.

The honest caveat: this isn't a Figma replacement. Complex component libraries, enterprise handoff workflows, design system governance at scale — it's not there yet. It lives at the ideation-to-validation stage, not the full design lifecycle.

But the conceptual reframe matters: when the prototype is the deliverable — the same HTML that will be handed off to a coding agent — the question "does it work?" moves to the beginning. That's not a small thing.
