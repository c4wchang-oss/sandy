## Topic 1: Builder.io Agent Native Design

**What:** Free, MIT-licensed, open-source HTML prototyping studio. The artifact you produce isn't a mockup — it's a running browser page.

**Source:** https://www.builder.io/blog/agent-native-design-figma-alternative

**Angle:** "The artifact is the screen, not a picture of the screen." Eliminates the mockup-to-reality gap at the ideation stage. Design system as generation source = your constraints are AI's quality ceiling.

**Honest caveat:** Not a Figma replacement. Complex component systems and enterprise governance aren't there yet. Ideation-to-browser validation only.

## Topic 2: Figma AI Impact Index 2026

**What:** Figma's annual measurement of AI's impact on design work. 62/100 in 2026 (nearly double 2024). 639 AI-moderated interviews, 3 years of survey data.

**Source:** https://www.figma.com/blog/measuring-the-impact-of-ai/

**Angle:** 62/100 = operational infrastructure threshold crossed. The real finding: "AI increased iteration speed but introduced quality concerns." Built the analysis dashboard using Figma's own MCP server — methodology IS the finding. Governance is now the senior designer's frontier.

**Honest caveat:** Figma measuring the impact of its own tools. Directional signal useful, absolute numbers require independent judgment.
