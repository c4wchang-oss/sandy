**Title:** 3 AI tools built the same self-review loop in 12 days. Here's what changed.

**Body:**

In the last 12 days, three separate AI creative tools built the same thing into their output pipeline: an automatic quality check before handing work back to the designer.

Oct 2: Shopify Canvas Sidekick screenshots its own output before returning it.
Oct 9: Gamma 5 checks for text spill, image crop, layout failures before presenting.
Oct 13: Automo checks every AI design for what a seasoned designer spots at a glance.

This is a pattern, not a coincidence. Here's what Automo specifically added and why it matters.

**What changed on Oct 13:**

1️⃣ **Structural quality auto-check** — Before you see the design, it's already been checked for: headlines hidden under shapes, button text overflowing, half-empty panels, dead space, placeholder copy. Caught and fixed before delivery.

2️⃣ **Brand constraints travel to Claude** — Claude connected to Automo now uses the same brand engine as native Mo: brand kit, templates, art direction, automatic fixes. Brand constraints aren't tool-specific anymore. They follow the AI assistant.

3️⃣ **Direction comparison in dashboard** — Generate two design directions side by side. Compare, then choose. Not "generate and iterate" — "compare two informed directions and commit."

4️⃣ **Designs with an idea behind them** — AI designer starts from a concept, uses the full frame, removes web UI clutter from print formats. Medium-specific constraint architecture, running automatically.

**The two-tier review model (now product architecture):**
- Layer 1 (agent handles): overflow, dead space, placeholder text, layout failures
- Layer 2 (you handle): wrong hierarchy, wrong emphasis, wrong emotional register, wrong IA for the audience

Automated floor. Judgment ceiling. Both necessary. Only one is automatable.

**Why this changes your review meetings:**
The agent self-review loop becoming standard means your review meeting starts at a different quality floor. Less time catching structural slop. More time on the judgment layer that only a senior designer can provide.

That's where your value is. Not catching button overflow.
