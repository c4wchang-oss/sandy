# Threads Post — Automo.ai AI Designer Everywhere + Self-Review Loop (English)

---

**Long-form version:**

I've been tracking agent self-review loops in AI creative tools for two weeks. Three instances in twelve days is enough to call it a pattern.

Shopify Canvas, October 2: Sidekick AI screenshots its own work before handing it back. Baked into the output pipeline, not announced as a feature.

Gamma 5, October 9: the rebuilt agent runs automated checks for text spill, image crop failures, and layout problems before presenting the result.

Automo, October 13: designs made by Mo, Claude connected to Automo, and the in-app AI designer are automatically checked for the mistakes a seasoned designer spots at a glance — headlines hidden under shapes, button text overflowing, half-empty panels, dead space, placeholder copy. Each one caught and sent back for fixing before you see it.

Three different tools. Three different categories (commerce, presentation, graphic design). Same design decision: the agent reviews its own work before presenting. This is becoming the expected behavior for any serious AI creative tool.

But the more interesting October 13 update is this: Claude connected to Automo now uses the same brand constraint engine as the native Mo designer. Brand kit, template references, art direction, automatic fixes — all of it travels to Claude. Brand constraints aren't tool-specific anymore. They follow the AI assistant.

This extends the reference-beats-description pattern to the real-time AI design loop. The constraint isn't a prompt. It's the brand kit itself. The agent works within it regardless of which AI assistant it's running through.

The direction comparison is also worth noting: generate from the dashboard and you now see two directions side by side before going further. That's not "generate and iterate." That's "compare two informed directions and choose." Small UX difference. Real process difference.

The honest caveat: automated quality checks catch structural problems. They don't catch wrong hierarchy, wrong emphasis, wrong emotional tone, or wrong IA for the audience. The quality floor is automated. The ceiling isn't. Don't confuse absence of slop for presence of taste.

---

**Short punchy take:**

The two-tier review model is now product architecture, not a workflow habit.

Layer 1 (agent): overflow, dead space, placeholder text, layout failures.
Layer 2 (designer): wrong hierarchy, wrong emphasis, wrong emotional register, wrong IA.

Both necessary. Only one is automatable. Know which one is yours.
