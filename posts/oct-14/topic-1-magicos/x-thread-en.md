**Tweet 1 (Hook)**
Design tools save your output.

---

**Tweet 2**
MagicOS is an infinite canvas where your PRD, inspiration images, design system, rejected directions, and final picks all live together — at once, not stacked on top of each other and lost.

This is a direct answer to the session boundary problem in multi-session AI design work.

---

**Tweet 3**
The architecture:
- The canvas: everything is a file (designs, docs, images, GitHub repos, notes)
- The coordinator: one chat that routes real work into threads
- Threads: agents doing one piece of work each, running in parallel

You talk to the coordinator. It spawns agents. Results appear on the canvas.

---

**Tweet 4**
The multi-agent pattern here is more structured than anything I've seen in a design tool.

PixelCrew (Sep 22) had specialist roles (researcher / director / builder) with real handoffs.
MagicOS has coordinator + threads — same pattern, but built into the product architecture rather than a one-off workflow.

**Tweet 5**

**Tweet 6**

**Tweet 7**
Honest take: the coordinator spawning parallel threads is powerful for divergence. Not necessarily for consistency.

5 threads working at once = 5 design directions that may not coherently resolve. Convergence is a judgment problem. The coordinator doesn't solve that — you do.
