**[Tweet 1 — Hook]**
Zeplin launched AI-generated screen flows in beta.

The 20-minute flow done in under a minute is not the story.

---

**[Tweet 2]**
How it works:

Drop your screens onto the flow canvas. Click "Create Flow with AI." Describe the flow you want in plain language.

AI puts the screens in order, connects them, labels the connectors with what moves the user forward.

It reads screen names, layers, and components — not just pixels. So your naming quality is your brief quality.

---

**[Tweet 3]**
The edge case story from Zeplin's own testing:

"An error path with no screen, a dead end with no way back, an edge case we mentioned but never designed."

This is what it surfaces. Not just the happy path you already knew. The gaps in the user journey you told yourself you'd handle later.

Catching those before your developer asks is not a small thing.

---

**[Tweet 4]**
What this is really doing:

Flagging missing coverage is expensive senior designer time. It's the gap analysis pass that happens right before dev handoff when someone finally asks "wait, what happens when the user enters an invalid input on step 4?"

AI Flows is making that pass cheaper. Not free — you still decide what to do about each gap. But cheaper.

---

**[Tweet 5]**
The brief quality ceiling applies here, but at the naming layer:

AI reads screen names, layer names, and component structure. If your screens are labeled "Screen 32" and "New Frame 7," the AI doesn't know what state they represent.

Well-named screens → useful flow. Poorly named screens → AI can connect what's there, can't surface what's missing if the concept isn't in the name.

Name your screens like you're briefing a junior designer who can't see your face.

---

**[Tweet 6]**
The production use case most people overlook:

"Tidy up a flow that's gotten messy."

When a feature expanded over four sprints. When three designers contributed different naming conventions. When the flow has 40 disconnected variations nobody can navigate.

AI Flows works on existing flows too. Drop a new screen in, describe how it fits, it inserts it with connectors and labels without you reshuffling everything.

**[Tweet 7]**
Availability and pricing:

AI Flows is in beta on all plans. No pricing changes while it's in beta.

Practically: this is worth running against any existing complex flow you've been meaning to audit. The gap report alone is worth the 10 minutes.

---

**[Tweet 8]**
The reframe:

The value of AI Flows is not the speed. Happy path documentation was always possible — just tedious.

The value is the gap report. The edge cases the AI surfaces that you haven't designed yet.

Run AI Flows not to generate your documentation. Run it to find out what you haven't documented yet.
