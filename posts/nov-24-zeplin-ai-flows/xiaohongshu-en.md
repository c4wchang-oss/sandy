## POST TITLE
Zeplin AI Flows catches the edge cases you forgot to design. That's the actual story.

---

## POST BODY

Zeplin launched AI Flows in beta (Nov 9). Drop your screens onto the flow canvas, describe the flow in plain language, and AI generates screen ordering, connectors, and transition labels in under a minute.

The speed benefit is real. But it's not the interesting part.

Here's what Zeplin's own team found during testing: *"An error path with no screen. A dead end with no way back. An edge case we mentioned but never designed."*

The AI flagged those gaps — not because it's smarter than the team, but because it's looking at the full graph of screens and surfacing where connections lead nowhere.

**What AI Flows actually does:**

→ Connects your screens in logical order based on what you describe
→ Labels connectors with what moves the user forward
→ Flags where there's a gap — a state you described but have no screen for
→ Works on existing messy flows too — add a screen, describe where it fits, AI connects it without you reshuffling

**5 things worth knowing:**

1. **The gap report is the value** — Run AI Flows not to generate documentation, but to discover what you haven't designed yet. The speed is a side effect.

2. **Screen naming is your brief** — AI reads screen names, layer names, and component structure. "Screen 32" tells it nothing. "Password Reset — Error State" tells it a lot. Brief quality is naming quality here.

3. **In beta on all plans, free during beta** — No pricing changes while in beta. Worth running against any complex flow you've been meaning to review.

4. **Works on existing flows** — Drop a new screen on an existing flow, describe where it belongs, AI inserts it with connectors and labels. No manual reshuffling.

5. **You still decide what to do with each gap** — AI surfaces the missing edge case. It doesn't design the screen. You decide whether it needs a screen, a placeholder, or is out of scope for this release.

**The honest reframe:**

Senior designers spend a lot of time on the gap analysis pass right before dev handoff — the "wait, what happens when...?" conversation. AI Flows makes that conversation cheaper to have.

The bottleneck moves from generating the happy path documentation (easy) to deciding how to handle the gaps it surfaces (still yours to judge).
