# 小紅書 Post — HeyVigo Infinite Canvas: Why AI Video's Real Bottleneck Is Context, Not Model Quality (English)
**Topic:** HeyVigo launches Infinite Canvas — node-based workspace for multimodal AI video production (Sep 28, 2026)

---

**Title:**
AI video's real problem isn't the model — it's that you can't track what you changed and why

---

**Body:**

HeyVigo just launched Infinite Canvas. It's not a new video generation model. It's the first tool I've seen built specifically to solve AI video production's actual bottleneck: context management at campaign scale.

Picture this: you're producing 200 video assets for a campaign. You have source images, brand overlays, copy variants, music moods. You run generation after generation across multiple sessions. A week later, a stakeholder asks you to update the hero product shot. You have no way to know which generated clips used it. You re-upload, re-prompt, and re-QA everything by memory.

That's not a model problem. That's a missing production relationship map.

**What Infinite Canvas actually does:**
Image, video, text, and audio nodes on an open workspace, connected to each other. Source material flows into generated assets, which flow into reviewed outputs, which flow into approved finals. The canvas is the production relationship map — not a folder of files.

**① Connected nodes = traceable production relationships**
Change a source reference → see which downstream nodes are connected to it. Not a new concept in design (this is literally what design system token relationships do), but brand new for AI video production.

**② Human-controlled modifications — the right trust model**
AI proposes canvas changes. You confirm before they execute. Read-only analysis (browsing, understanding) runs immediately. This is the correct split: modification needs a confirm gate, analysis doesn't. Same logic as Figma Make drafts and Cursor Auto-review.

**③ Reusable assets that actually stay connected**
Stop re-uploading the same brand assets every session. Source nodes live on the canvas and stay linked to everything that references them.

**④ Built for production scale — not casual creation**
Designed for ad campaigns, e-commerce product videos, storyboards, short dramas. Not a beginner tool. The node-based workflow has real cognitive overhead that pays off at campaign scale, not for weekly social posts.

**The honest caveats:**
HeyVigo is from Axon AI Ltd (Ras Al Khaimah) — no major brand recognition. No published model quality data or benchmarks. Infinite canvas is a crowded pattern — Figma, Miro, Doop, and Mirage Tesseract all have versions. You need an account and model access varies by plan.

**The convergence this closes:**
Doop (Sep 4) → canvas as persistent AI design context
Mirage Tesseract (Sep 23) → agent-native video engine with primitive access
HeyVigo (Sep 28) → spatial production relationship map for video

All three solving the same session boundary problem at different scales.

---

**Honest caveat:**
New company, unproven model quality, unresolved pricing details. Node-based workflow has overhead — best for large-scale production teams, not small creators.

**The reframe:**
Every AI video tool is racing on model quality. HeyVigo is betting that the campaign-scale production organization problem will matter more. They're probably both right. The best tool will solve both. Infinite Canvas is the first serious attempt at the organization half.
