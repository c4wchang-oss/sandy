# X Thread — HeyVigo Infinite Canvas: The Session Boundary Problem for AI Video Production (English)
**Topic:** HeyVigo launches Infinite Canvas — node-based workspace for multimodal AI video creation (Sep 28, 2026)
**Angle:** The bottleneck for AI video production was never model quality. It was context — how production assets, decisions, and references stay connected across a project. Infinite Canvas is the first dedicated attempt to solve this for video.

---

**Tweet 1 / Hook**
Every AI video tool tells you about model quality.

HeyVigo Infinite Canvas tells you about workflow organization.

That's either the right bet — or a way to avoid talking about model quality.

---

**Tweet 2**
The core problem Infinite Canvas is solving:

AI video generation is non-linear. An idea branches into references, alternates, revisions, and final assets. But every AI tool treats the session as a flat list of prompts with generated outputs.

You end up managing context in your head. Or in separate Notion docs. Or by re-uploading source images into every new session.

---

**Tweet 3**
Infinite Canvas organizes the production as a relationship map.

Image, video, text, and audio nodes on an open workspace. Nodes connected to each other — so you can see how a source image flows through to a generated scene, through a revision, into a final output.

Change the source. See what else changes.

---

**Tweet 4**
The "human-controlled changes" model is the part that matters most.

AI can propose canvas modifications. The user confirms before they execute. Read-only analysis (browsing, understanding) runs immediately without approval.

This is the correct trust split. The same logic as Figma Make's draft model, Elementor MCP's editor draft, Cursor Auto-review's classifier — production modifications need a confirm gate. Analysis doesn't.

---

**Tweet 5**
The reusable assets pattern closes a real production loop.

Stop uploading the same brand product photo into every AI session. The source asset lives on the canvas. Every node that references it updates when it changes.

This is the same relationship problem design systems solved for UI. AI video production is 3-4 years behind on this infrastructure.
