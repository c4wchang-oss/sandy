**Title:**
This AI design tool outputs files you can actually edit (and it matters)

---

**Body:**

Finally. An AI tool that doesn't give you a flat image.

Linearity AI (Curve 7.0) just launched and it works differently from every other AI design tool. Instead of generating pixels, it generates design structure.

**What that means in practice:**
① Text stays live and retypeable after generation
② Colors are real brand variables — not pixel approximations
③ Every layer is editable: vectors, images, layout
④ Resize to any format without regenerating
⑤ Translate copy without rebuilding the whole file

**What it's actually for:**
Social ads, campaign banners, landing page visuals, print materials. This is a brand/marketing design tool — not product UI, not component libraries.

**What the brief quality ceiling looks like:**
The model uses your brand kit: colors, fonts, imagery, tone of voice. If that's vague or incomplete, the output is editable but wrong. Your brand brief quality is your ceiling, not the model.

**Why this is a bigger deal than it looks:**
We've been watching the "flat PNG problem" across all AI creative tools — every generator produced dead-end outputs you couldn't build on. Linearity Vector 1.0 is the first design model that generates structure from the start. This completes a pattern across every creative medium.

**Honest take:**
It's not Figma. It's for the brand/marketing design layer. But for that use case, this is the first time AI output = working file, not just a starting point.

**Rating: 4/5**
Strong for brand design. Scope is narrow. Brief quality is still yours to own.
