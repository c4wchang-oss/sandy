## Topic: Dscout MCP — Research Embedded in the AI Workflow

---

**Title:** User research just moved inside the AI workflow. Here's what that actually means.

**Body:**

Dscout launched an MCP this week.

From inside Claude, GitHub Copilot, or Figma Make, you can now:
→ Draft a user research study
→ Launch it to participants
→ Get back synthesized findings

Without switching tools.

This is bigger than "faster research."

**Here's the real shift:**

**1. Research used to live in a separate layer**
Dedicated tool, dedicated person, dedicated timeline. MCP collapses the distance. Research now happens inside the same workspace where you're designing or coding.

**2. The same pattern is repeating everywhere**
ProtoPie MCP → ProtoPie reads your prototype directly.
Dovetail Channels → Research insights feed into agent workflows.
Usertour MCP → Onboarding built by AI from a brief.
Now Dscout → User feedback triggered from Claude.

Every research touchpoint is becoming an agent endpoint.

**3. The cost gate just disappeared**
"Can we afford this study?" used to be the bottleneck. That question is gone.

"Did we ask the right question?" is the only one left.

**4. The failure mode is subtle and real**
AI delivers confident-sounding answers fast.
If your brief is vague, you get confident-sounding vague results. Faster.

That looks like evidence when it isn't. Worse than no research.

**5. The role shift for senior researchers**
You're not the person who runs studies and writes reports anymore.

You're the person who designs the research system:
→ Question architecture
→ Study sequencing
→ Synthesis criteria

Those are the new deliverables. The report is what the agent produces.

**The bottom line:**
Proximity to the decision matters more than research ceremony.

But only if you've designed the inputs.

Your brief is now your spec for the AI. Don't write it casually.
