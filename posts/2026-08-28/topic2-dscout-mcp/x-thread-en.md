# X Thread — English
## Topic: Dscout MCP — Research Embedded in the AI Workflow

---

**Tweet 1 (hook)**
Dscout shipped an MCP this week.

Which means you can now draft, launch, and get back user research findings without ever leaving Claude.

That's not a workflow speed-up.
That's a workflow collapse.

---

**Tweet 2**
The old model:

Design in Figma → open research tool → write study → recruit → wait → synthesize → present.

The MCP model:

Describe what you want to learn → agent drafts study → launches it → returns findings → back to designing.

---

**Tweet 3**
Same pattern keeps repeating:
ProtoPie MCP. Dovetail Channels. Usertour MCP. Now Dscout.

Every research touchpoint is becoming an agent endpoint.

Research isn't getting faster.
It's getting embedded.

---

**Tweet 4**
What doesn't change: brief quality is still the ceiling.

"Can we afford this research?" used to be the bottleneck.
That question is basically gone now.

"Did we ask the right question?" is the only one left.
And it's harder than it sounds.

---

**Tweet 5**
The failure mode to watch:

AI-embedded research generates answers. It just generates them faster.

If your study brief is vague, you'll get confident-sounding vague results.
Faster.

That's a new kind of bad. Worse than no research because it feels like research.

---

**Tweet 6**
The senior researcher role doesn't disappear.
It moves upstream.

You stop being the person who runs studies and reports findings.

You become the person who designs the research system the AI executes.

---

**Tweet 7**
That means:

→ Study brief architecture
→ Question taxonomy
→ Participant recruitment logic
→ Synthesis criteria

Those are the new primary deliverables.
Not the report.

---

**Tweet 8**
Every MCP that embeds research into an AI workflow is making the same bet:

Proximity to the decision matters more than research ceremony.

I think they're right.
But only if you've designed the inputs.

Brief quality = the only gate left.
