## Content

The "agent edits the wrong component" problem just got a proper solution.

Ilse is a toolbar that overlays on your running React app. Click an element, describe the change, and your coding agent receives the exact file, line number, and design tokens — not your description of them.

**How it works in practice:**

1. **Open your app** — `npx ilse-design@latest` in your project folder. Detects Next.js, Vite, Remix, Astro automatically.
2. **Point and say** — Click any element, write what should change. Agent gets the precise context, not a paragraph of words.
3. **Panel tweaks** — Spacing, typography, colors with your own design tokens. Simple changes land instantly, zero AI, zero tokens spent.
4. **Component library** — Ilse auto-generates `DESIGN.md` from your components and tokens. Your agent stops inventing new buttons.

**Pro (pre-sale, ships November):**
Test mode — describe a user journey ("buy the lamp"), Ilse walks your real app and reports what breaks.

**Why this matters for UX designers:**
The "visual pointing as agent context" pattern is now confirmed three times in 2026: MagicPath (canvas), Skisso (live CSS), Ilse (dev app). Every time, it outperforms text description for precision.

**The honest read:**
- React only
- Requires your own coding agent (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Gemini)
- Pro is pre-sale; test mode ships November 2026
- Very early, solo founder, but the problem statement is one of the most precise of the year

Built by a designer who spent 9 years in design before moving to code. The frustration is real. The solution is direct.
