**Title:** This AI design tool did what most others won't: kept every stage of the design process

**Body:**

I've tried a lot of AI design tools. They all have one problem in common: they skip stages.

Give it a prompt, get a mockup, "design complete."

PixelCrew is different.

**Three agents. Three roles. Real handoffs.**

**Elena** — The Researcher
Reads your brief, does market and user research, outputs a research report

**Marcus** — The Creative Director
Writes three creative direction pitches, then argues for the strongest one (and yes, it takes a real position)

**Mira** — The Designer
Builds wireframes, outputs production-ready HTML with a design system

You can watch them discuss decisions as they work. Nothing is a black box.

**Each brief takes 25–45 minutes**

Not instant — and that's actually a good sign. It means the pipeline is real, not pretend.

**The detail I care about most**

Marcus doesn't just give you three options. He argues for one.

That's the model making a taste judgment on your behalf. Examine it — don't accept it blindly.

**Why structure beats the "one smart agent" approach**

Specialized multi-agent > one generalist agent

Same reason a well-structured team outperforms a single genius individual. But you need enough design judgment to evaluate what each agent actually delivered.
