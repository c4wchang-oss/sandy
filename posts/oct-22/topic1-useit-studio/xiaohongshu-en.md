**Title:**
UseIt Studio: The AI design tool that finally understands physical constraints

---

**Body:**

As a senior UX designer, I've seen a lot of "AI design tools" this year. Most generate a nice flat image and leave you to reconstruct everything.

UseIt Studio is different. It's built for physical design — packaging, stickers, pins, gift boxes — and outputs real, editable files, not PNGs.

---

**What it actually solves:**

① **Manufacturing constraints**
Bleed, dielines, cut lines for shaped pieces, white ink layers for acrylic — these are production requirements, not aesthetic choices. UseIt handles them natively. Most AI tools pretend they don't exist.

② **The "change this, keep the rest" problem**
Select one element, describe the change in plain language, only that updates. The surrounding design holds. Sounds basic. Most AI editors still regenerate the whole canvas. UseIt gets this right from v1.

③ **3D preview before committing**
How does the gift box look when folded? Is the white ink layer positioned correctly on the acrylic piece? UseIt shows a live 3D preview so you can confirm before sending to print.

④ **Continues in Illustrator**
The .ai file output is fully editable in Illustrator, including conversation-driven editing inside that environment. Not a dead export — a starting position.

---

**Honest limits:**
- Early beta — limited to gift boxes / stickers / pins / pop-up cards / brand kits
- Not production-scale packaging
- Windows desktop app + browser version

---

**Senior UX take:**

The founder's note: "the agent works directly in design software instead of generating a picture."

That single sentence is the whole product philosophy. This year, the editable-output convergence — images, animation, video, brand design structure — UseIt closes it on physical design.
