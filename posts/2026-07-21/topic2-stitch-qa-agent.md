# Topic 2: Google Stitch July 2026 — AI as Design QA Agent
**Date:** July 21, 2026
**Core Insight:** AI is moving from being a design generator to a design completion and audit layer. Stitch's new features (missing states, accessibility audit, animation, marketing kit, instant prototype) are all about completing existing work — not generating new work.
**Hook:** Everyone asked "can AI design?" The more interesting question is "can AI complete a design that already exists?"

## THREADS (ENGLISH)

Everyone's been asking "can AI design?" for the past two years.

Google Stitch just started answering a different question: "can AI complete a design that already exists?"

The July update to Stitch adds: generate missing states from existing screens, animate static UI, run an accessibility audit, generate a marketing kit from a screen, convert static to interactive prototype.

None of these are generation features. They're completion features.

And completion is where professional design work actually lives. Generation gets you to 60% — the idea, the rough layout, the visual structure. The other 40% is the work that takes 3x as long: missing states, edge cases, accessibility, error handling, empty states.

The missing states feature is the one I keep coming back to. AI looking at your existing screen and identifying structural gaps — "you have a loaded state, but no empty state, no error state, no skeleton" — that's not a generator. That's the senior design review you don't always have bandwidth for.

Honest take: Stitch output still isn't production-quality. You'll clean things up after every generation. But the audit capabilities are genuinely useful in early-to-mid sprint, even if the generation is just scaffolding.

The shift I'm watching: AI moves from input device to QA layer. That's when it becomes infrastructure.

The "AI can generate UI" narrative is getting boring. Google Stitch just launched something more interesting: AI that audits and completes your existing designs.

🔷 4. Marketing Kit

🔷 5. Instant Prototype
Convert any static screen into a simple interactive flow. Good for quick stakeholder presentations without rebuilding in prototype mode.

Designers skip missing states not because they don't know how — but because deadline pressure cuts them. An AI that flags "you have a loaded state but no empty state" changes the incentive structure. QA happens earlier, in context, before the sprint ends.
