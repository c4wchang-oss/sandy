Image generators (Midjourney, Flux, GPT Image) produce pixels. Beautiful and fast, but flat.

One file you can't open. Change the headline → generate again → layout shifts → approved design lost. Every designer knows this loop.

**Tweet 3**
Vyb works in an internal language called Cocolang. Text stays a text object. Your logo stays a vector. Product photo keeps your original pixels at your resolution.

Each layer is independent. Change the headline — only the headline changes.

**Tweet 4**
The key mechanism: Vyb takes your actual brand assets as direct input.

Your font file, your product photography, your color values. It doesn't approximate your brand from training data. It composes *with* your assets. Big difference.
