**Title:** AI image generators vs. AI design models — why the difference matters for your brand

---

You've tried AI image generators for design work. Every time you change one word, the whole layout shifts. Your brand fonts get replaced. Your product photo quality drops. You regenerate. The approved layout is gone.

**Vyb (Coco Labs)** launched yesterday. It's a different type of AI tool: a design model, not an image generator.

Here's the actual difference:

---

**Image generator:**
→ Produces pixels that look like design
→ Learns patterns from training data (reads: copies industry median)
→ Your brand = approximate match at best
→ Output = flat file, nothing editable

**Design model (Vyb):**
→ Composes in layers using your actual assets
→ Text stays text, vector stays vector, photos stay your original pixels
→ Your brand = your exact font, colors, photography used directly
→ Output = editable stack of layers

---

**How Vyb works:**

1. Give it your brand assets (font files, product photos, color values)
2. Write a prompt for your campaign (social post, banner, slide, email)
3. Vyb builds the composition in its own format (Cocolang), preserving every layer
4. Export to Figma or other design tools — each element on its own editable layer
5. Change the headline: only the headline changes. Nothing else moves.

---

**The "taste" claim:**
Vyb says it learns which compositions you'd actually approve — not just execute a brand kit mechanically. This is the hardest part to verify but the most consequential if true.

---

**The tech question for industry watchers:**
Cocolang (Vyb's intermediate format) is a new bet in a space where:
- Figma uses a proprietary binary format (great for design, opaque to AI)
- Elyx (Sketch founders) bets on human-readable plain text in Git
- Vyb bets on a model-native language both AI and humans can edit

Three different answers to: why doesn't design have a standard format?
