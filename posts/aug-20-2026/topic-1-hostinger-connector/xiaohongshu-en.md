**Title:** Hostinger AI Builder launched | Every platform now has a Connector. What should designers understand?

---

This week I came across a launch worth unpacking: Hostinger AI Builder.

On the surface it's an AI website builder. But buried inside it is something more relevant to designers: Connector.

**What is Connector?**
An open API that lets Claude Code, Cursor, and VS Code operate directly against your hosting account — deploy a project, connect a domain, update inventory, send email campaigns.

No tool switching. No manual handoff. Same session handles it all.

---

**Why does this matter?**

This isn't just a website builder upgrade. It's part of a larger pattern spreading across platforms:

- Figma MCP → AI runs design tools directly
- ProtoPie MCP → AI reads your prototype
- Hostinger Connector → AI deploys your site

**Every platform is becoming an AI endpoint.**

The design → deploy gap is collapsing.

---

**What this means for designers:**

1. Your brief is no longer just a human-readable document. It needs to be something an AI can read, plan from, and execute.
2. Constraint architecture and system legibility matter more than ever
3. "Designing for handoff" is ending. "Designing for agent execution" is starting.

---

**Honest caveat:**

This makes no-code faster and more connected. But good product design hasn't changed — you still need to know what you're building, for whom, and why.

AI can execute the plan. It still can't write the brief that makes execution worth anything.
