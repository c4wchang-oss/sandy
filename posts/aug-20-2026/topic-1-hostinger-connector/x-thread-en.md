**Tweet 1 (hook):**
Your design file can now deploy itself.

Not hyperbole. This week Hostinger launched AI Builder with something called Connector — an open API that lets Claude Code, Cursor, and VS Code operate against your hosting account directly.

A thread on what this means for how we work. 🧵

---

**Tweet 2:**
The thing Hostinger actually shipped isn't a drag-and-drop builder.

It's a platform that treats your entire online project — site, app, auth, database, ecommerce, email — as an agent-addressable API.

That's structurally different from "AI helps you build a website."

---

**Tweet 3:**
Connector means: the same external AI assistant writing your code can also deploy it, connect a domain, update your inventory, and draft a campaign.

No handoff. No context switch. Same session.

The design → deploy loop just got one click shorter — or rather, one human shorter.

---

**Tweet 4:**
For UX designers, the relevant question isn't "can I build a site now?"

It's: what does your role own when the design artifact → live infrastructure gap collapses?

Brief quality. Constraint architecture. System legibility.

Those move upstream. Not away.

---

**Tweet 5:**
The input types tell the real story:
- Upload a menu PDF → AI builds your restaurant site
- Paste a screenshot → site is rebuilt from the image
- Link an existing site → AI rebuilds it from scratch

The brief is whatever you have. The AI figures out the rest.

---

**Tweet 6:**
This is the Connector pattern, and it's spreading:

Figma MCP → run design tools from Claude/ChatGPT
ProtoPie MCP → AI reads your prototype directly
Hostinger Connector → AI deploys your site directly

Every platform is becoming an agent endpoint.

---

**Tweet 7:**
The honest caveat: this makes no-code faster and more connected. But it doesn't change what good product design is.

You still have to know what you're building, for whom, and why.

AI can execute the plan. It still can't write the brief that makes execution worth anything.

---

**Tweet 8 (CTA):**
When every platform has a Connector, the designer's job is writing agent-legible briefs.

Not "design for handoff." Not "design for dev."

Design for agents that can read, execute, and deploy from the same source of truth.

That's the new version of systems thinking.
