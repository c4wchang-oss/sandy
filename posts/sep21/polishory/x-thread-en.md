Visual feedback linked to screenshots. Prioritized recommendations for spacing, layout, typography, visual hierarchy. No source code required.

**Tweet 5**
What automated audits can score:
— spacing consistency
— layout rhythm and density
— typography scale
— visual hierarchy clarity
— color contrast

What they can't:
— whether the interaction earns trust
— whether the IA is right for this audience's decision context
— whether the visual personality is appropriate or just generic-good

**Tweet 6**

**Tweet 7**
Quality review is splitting into two tiers:

1. Automated floor enforcement — mechanical checks (spacing, contrast, hierarchy legibility)
2. Judgment-based ceiling work — does this earn trust, is this the right IA, does the personality fit?

The first tier is being automated. The second is what senior designers actually do.
