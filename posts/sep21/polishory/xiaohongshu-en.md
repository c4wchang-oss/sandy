**Title: There's now a tool that audits AI-generated websites. Here's what it can and can't do.**

---

AI generates the UI. Who reviews the AI?

---

## What Polishory does

Polishory scans any public URL — desktop and mobile — and returns:
- Design scorecard
- Visual feedback linked to screenshots
- Prioritized recommendations for spacing, layout, typography, visual hierarchy
- No source code required

Built during the OpenAI GPT-6 Astra Challenge in 3 days. Launched September 19, 2026.

---

## The problem it solves

Most AI builders have zero quality review layer. You describe what you want. AI builds it. You ship it.

Polishory gives solo founders and indie creators a design quality reference frame they wouldn't otherwise have.

---

## What automated audits CAN evaluate

✓ Spacing consistency
✓ Layout rhythm and visual density
✓ Typography scale, sizing, and contrast
✓ Visual hierarchy clarity
✓ Color contrast ratios

---

## What automated audits CANNOT evaluate

✗ Whether the interaction earns trust for first-time users
✗ Whether the information architecture fits this audience's decision context
✗ Whether the visual personality is appropriate or just generically competent
✗ Whether complex interactions are comprehensible under time pressure
✗ Whether the copy tone matches the product stage and audience

---

## The meta-layer worth noting

Polishory was built in 3 days using GPT-6 Astra.

The tool diagnosing AI slop was built with the same class of tool that produces it.

That's fine. The constraint logic defining "quality" is still human-authored. That part doesn't automate.

---

## Two tiers of quality review

| Tier | What it checks | Automated? |
|------|---------------|-----------|
| Floor | Spacing, contrast, hierarchy, density | ✅ Yes (Polishory) |
| Ceiling | Trust, IA fit, personality, context | ❌ No (still yours) |

---

## Honest assessment

✅ Free for public URLs
✅ No source code required
✅ Desktop + mobile analysis
✅ Good starting point for solo builders with no design background
⚠️ Scores mechanical signals, not experiential quality
⚠️ Built during a challenge — expect rough edges
⚠️ Directional review layer, not a design review replacement

---

## The takeaway

Quality review is splitting into two tiers.

The floor is now automated for anyone with a URL.

The ceiling — earning trust, choosing the right IA, fitting the right personality to the right context — is still what you're paid for.

If you can't articulate the difference between the two, that's worth working on.
