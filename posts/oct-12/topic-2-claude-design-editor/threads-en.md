# Threads Post — Claude Design Editor Controls + Oct 15 (English)

---

**Long-form version:**

The pattern I've been tracking since September just completed another loop.

Claude Design started as a standalone site for generating static mockups. It's now an element-editable canvas, embedded in every Claude conversation and every Claude Code terminal session, connected to 14 downstream tools, and turning on by default for enterprise teams in three days.

The editor controls matter more than people are giving them credit for. Drag, resize, align, inline text edit, adjustment sliders for spacing and color — this closes the "generate and immediately lose creative control" failure mode that's made AI design tools frustrating for anyone who actually cares about craft. You're not committing to whatever the model makes. You can refine on canvas, then hand off to implementation.

The /design command in Claude Code is where this gets interesting for how I actually work. You're in the terminal, building something. You type /design, Claude generates artboards against your design system, you open the canvas in a browser, you edit, and you push it directly back to Claude Code for implementation. Design is happening inside the development workflow now, not upstream of it in a disconnected tool.

The export connections — Adobe, Canva, Gamma, HyperFrames, Lovable, Miro, Netlify, Replit, v0, Vercel, Wix, 14 total — are what makes this a platform move rather than a feature update. Your design system becomes the portable constraint that travels with the design to every connected tool. That's the design.md portability thesis from September executed at platform scale.

October 15 is the governance moment. Every enterprise team gets this enabled by default. If you're a design leader and you haven't looked at your design system's readiness for this, you have three days. The quality of what your org's Claude produces is directly tied to how good your design system documentation is. This isn't a technical problem. It's a DesignOps problem.

The honest complexity: "accessible to everyone" and "used well by everyone" are different problems. The governance gap opens the moment access becomes universal.

---

**Short punchy take:**

Claude Design grew up.

The question isn't whether AI can generate a mockup anymore.

The question is whether your design system is good enough to be the constraint layer for all of it.

October 15. Three days. Check your design system.
