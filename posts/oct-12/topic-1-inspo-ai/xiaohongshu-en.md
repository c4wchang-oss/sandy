**Title:** Stop doing design research in 5 tabs | Inspo AI reviewed

**Body:**

If you're still doing your design references across Dribbble + Behance + Mobbin + Pinterest + Awwwards and then assembling screenshots into a Google Doc — this one's for you.

Inspo AI just launched. Here's the honest breakdown from someone who's done this research loop more times than I can count.

**What it does:**

1️⃣ **AI design search** — 50,000+ curated UI screens. Search in plain language design intent: "mobile checkout with generous whitespace, minimal iconography, warm palette." Gets reference-quality results, not keyword matches.

2️⃣ **Moodboard in same workspace** — Click any result → goes into your board. Add color palettes, font pairs, notes. Share via link. No export step, no screenshot chaos.

3️⃣ **Brand audit** — Upload brand guidelines + your design → get a score out of 100 with visual annotations pointing at the exact violations. Real brand QA, not vibes.

4️⃣ **Capture to Figma** — Send reference screens straight to Figma without leaving the workspace.

5️⃣ **AI UI gen** — Generate UI concepts from within the same tool, informed by the references you've already curated.

**Who it's for:**
Senior designers doing early-phase discovery. Product designers who need to move fast on client alignment. Anyone who's lost 2 hours to "tab archaeology" at the start of a project.

**Honest caveats:**
- 50,000 screens sounds impressive. Freshness and curation quality are what actually matter, and that's the open question.
- Works best when used for research → brief quality, NOT as a generation-first tool.
- The brand audit is the most differentiated feature. Worth testing that specifically.

**The bigger frame:**
Better references → better briefs → better AI output.
The quality ceiling for AI design work isn't about the model. It starts here.
