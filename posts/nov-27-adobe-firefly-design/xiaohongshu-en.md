**Adobe just collapsed 6 Creative Cloud apps into one workspace — here's what actually changed**

Adobe Firefly Design launched in beta on Nov 25. Here's what it actually means for production workflows:

**What Firefly Design is:**
One workspace combining raster editing, vector illustration, motion, 3D design, and print. Work that previously required switching between Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, and more.

Creative Agent (called "AI Assistants") is now rolling into all major CC apps: Premiere, Illustrator, InDesign, After Effects, Frame.io. Photoshop has it in a separate beta app.

**The 3 things worth your attention:**

1. **Plan mode** — Agent shows you its execution plan before doing anything. You review and approve (or switch to fully autonomous). This is the right trust model for production work. Brief quality = quality ceiling.

2. **Brand preferences saved in Firefly** — Upload your guidelines, colors, fonts, characters, products once. Agent reads them on every generation without re-prompting. Same pattern as Luma Brand Kit, Automo — brand constraints loaded once, applied automatically.

3. **Local MCP integrations (coming early 2027)** — External agents (Claude, Cursor) will connect directly to your Photoshop/Illustrator/Premiere project files on your own machine. Layers, vectors, sequences stay editable. This is the one to watch. When it ships, the production pipeline becomes fully agent-readable.

**What's still beta (= not GA, not stable):**
- Firefly Design: beta, shipping timeline unclear
- Creative Agent: beta capability levels vary by app
- Local MCP: private beta, targeting early 2027

**The honest frame:**
Brand preference saving is reference input, not constraint enforcement. The agent reads your guidelines — it doesn't architecturally prevent violations. And "AI Assistants" is a rebrand; not all of them use the Creative Agent yet.

The arc: web platform MCPs (Webflow → Framer → Elementor) closed in 46 days. Desktop creative production MCPs are the next wave. Firefly Design local MCP is the signal that this is real, not hypothetical.
