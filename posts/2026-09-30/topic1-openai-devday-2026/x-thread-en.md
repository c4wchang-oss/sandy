# X Thread — OpenAI DevDay 2026: ChatGPT Just Became a Design Collaboration Workspace (English)
**Topic:** OpenAI DevDay 2026 — Plugin Extensions with Figma inside ChatGPT, ChatGPT Dots always-on agents, Pages collaborative docs, MCP Events (Sep 29, 2026)
**Angle:** ChatGPT stopped being a chat window. Plugin Extensions mean Figma designs, comments, and files can now live inside the same conversation. The context organization problem that's been slowing down AI design workflows just got a first real answer.

---

**Tweet 1 / Hook**
OpenAI just ran DevDay 2026 with 20+ announcements.

The design one that matters isn't the headline.

It's the quiet shift from "ChatGPT as chat assistant" to "ChatGPT as collaborative workspace."

---

**Tweet 2**
Plugin Extensions change the interaction model.

Developers can now build full app-like experiences inside ChatGPT — sidebar panels, interactive UIs, custom file viewers.

The keynote demoed Figma designs and comments living in the same window as your ChatGPT conversation.

Not switching tabs. Same window.

---

**Tweet 3**
Think about what that means for the design review loop.

You open a design critique. Your Figma file, your comments, and your AI conversation are in one panel.

No more: export → paste → re-explain context → get feedback → switch back.

The context stays. The conversation compounds.

---

**Tweet 4**
ChatGPT Dots is the even bigger structural shift.

Always-on agents connected to 4,000+ apps — with their own cloud computers.

Your AI assistant now persists across your entire toolchain. Figma. Slack. Jira. Notion. You don't have to re-explain the project context every session.

This is what every design team has been manually duct-taping together with browser tabs.

---

**Tweet 5**
MCP Events makes the integration active, not passive.

Connected apps can now trigger automations. Design tool actions flow downstream automatically.

Figma status change → Jira ticket update → team Slack notification.

The handoff layer didn't disappear. It got automated.

**Tweet 6**
Pages is the piece to watch for research and documentation work.

Collaborative docs where AI and humans work in the same canvas — not AI generating a doc you paste, but actual co-authoring with live edits.

Research synthesis. Design rationale. Brief writing.

The artifact is the conversation now.

**Tweet 7**
The honest caveats:

Quality ceiling is still wherever your Figma file and project context are. ChatGPT Dots requires app connections that need to be built and maintained.

Plugin Extensions are only as good as the plugins someone built.

And 20 announcements in one day means rough edges across every surface area.

This is infrastructure. It takes months to understand what it actually changes.
