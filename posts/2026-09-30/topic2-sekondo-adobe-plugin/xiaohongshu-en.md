**Body:**

Every AI design tool launched in the last two years solves the same problem: generating new things.

Sekondo, launched today by a 34-year graphic design veteran, solves the different problem: working on production files that already exist.

Here's what it actually is and why it matters:

**What Sekondo is:**
A plugin that connects Claude or ChatGPT to all 6 Adobe Creative apps — Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, After Effects, Premiere Pro, and Lightroom Classic — and works inside your actual files. Real layers, real text styles, fully editable output. Uses your own Claude or ChatGPT subscription.

**① InDesign: the use case that converts skeptics**
12-page brochure adapted to 3 languages in 7 seconds. Frame by frame. Every text style preserved. In a normal studio pipeline that's 2-3 days. No independently verified benchmark yet, but if it holds under real conditions, that's a pipeline transformation for any studio doing international work.

**② Photoshop: batch retouching with consistent standards**
124 jewelry photos at 1 second each. The key isn't speed — it's consistency. AI applies the same eye across every frame in a shoot, which is what manual retouching loses at scale.

**③ The interaction model that earns trust**
Sekondo reads the document structure before touching anything. It proposes, you confirm. You sign off before execution. This is the right trust architecture for production work — "AI proposes, human decides" instead of "AI executes, human discovers."

**④ No proprietary model ceiling**
You use your own Claude or ChatGPT subscription. The quality ceiling is whichever frontier model you trust most. It improves every time Anthropic or OpenAI ships an update. No new AI bill. No vendor lock-in.

**⑤ Use cases across all 6 apps**
Illustrator: variation generation on existing brand assets
After Effects: expression writing in plain language (finally)
Premiere Pro: timeline gap filling and cut consistency
Lightroom: batch adjustments on curated export sets

**Honest caveat:**
Built by one person — support depth will be limited early. Quality ceiling is your brief quality; Sekondo doesn't know your brand standards unless you tell it. Demo numbers (7 seconds, 1 second/photo) are from demos, not independent testing. Free reservations at sekondo.app until delivery.

Adobe optimized for "what can AI generate?"
