### Topic 1: Replit Design
**Source:** https://replit.com/blog/introducing-replit-design (Jul 29, 2026)

**Core Insight:** The handoff didn't get better — it got eliminated. Replit Design removes the export/rebuild cycle by keeping design in context all the way to ship. The artifact IS the design IS the app.

**Key Facts:**
- Ambient Intelligence: suggests design variations/progressions before you ask
- Mobbin built-in: 600K+ screens from 1,000+ apps (no account needed)
- Design systems: upload/build, everything snaps to brand in one click
- Model choice: Claude, GPT-5, Gemini, Kimi, GLM
- Canvas frame → Build → live running app in one workflow

**Real Headline:** "Design and shipping are now the same step."

### Topic 2: Framer CanvasBench + Fable 5 / Opus 5
**Sources:**
- https://www.framer.com/canvasbench/ — CanvasBench benchmark
- https://www.framer.com/updates/fable-5 (Jul 29, 2026)
- https://www.framer.com/updates/opus-5 (Jul 28, 2026)

**Core Insight:** Framer built a benchmark for AI design tasks — 236 canvas challenges. Combined with Fable 5 (proactive, creative) and Opus 5 (efficient, matches Fable at half the credits), model selection in Framer is now a data-backed design decision, not a vibe.

**Key Facts:**
- CanvasBench: 236 canvas challenges; Navigation Benchmark is primary test
- Sol: 100% — baseline, fastest, most complete
- Terra: 94% — efficient
- Fable 5: 83%, design quality leader, "proactive" (goes beyond brief), handles shaders/animations, 2× Sol credits
- Opus 5: matches Fable 5 score at 0.5× credits, 1.5 min faster + 12% more efficient than Opus 4.8

**Real Headline:** "Framer defined what good AI design performance looks like. Model selection just got a rubric."
