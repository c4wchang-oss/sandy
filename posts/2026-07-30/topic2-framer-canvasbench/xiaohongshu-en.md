**Title:** Framer's real announcement wasn't new models — it was a design benchmark

---

Framer dropped Fable 5 and Opus 5 this week. Everyone's talking about the models. I'm talking about CanvasBench.

🔍 WHAT IS CANVASBENCH

236 Framer canvas challenges measuring:
① Layout accuracy
② Design understanding  
③ Edit quality
④ Web design workflows

Navigation Benchmark (hardest): turn a non-functional nav design into a fully responsive interactive nav with mega menus + mobile sliding drawer.

📊 THE MODEL LEADERBOARD

Sol: 100% — baseline, fastest, most complete
Terra: 94% — efficient, fewer credits
Fable 5: 83% — design quality leader, proactive, handles shaders + animations
Opus 5: matches Fable 5 at 0.5× credits, 12% more efficient than previous

🎨 WHY FABLE 5 IS INTERESTING

It's "proactive" — goes beyond the brief, sets up styles, reuses elements across the whole site. That's not just generation quality. It's design systems thinking baked into model behavior.

⚡ THE PRACTICAL MODEL GUIDE

→ Hero work, quality is the only metric → Fable 5
→ Speed & budget matter → Opus 5 or Terra
→ Bulk / consistency → Sol

💡 THE REAL SHIFT

Framer defined what "AI design quality" means on a canvas. Not code scores. Canvas-native tasks, scored on design outcomes. Model selection just became a data-backed design decision, not a vibe.

🎯 REAL HEADLINE

Not "two new models."
"Framer built the rubric for AI design performance."
Model awareness is now a design skill with evidence.
