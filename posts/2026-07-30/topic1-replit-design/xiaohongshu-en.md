**Title:** Replit Design launched — no handoff, no export, just ship

---

Replit just dropped Replit Design and everyone's calling it "Figma for coders." That's the wrong read.

🔍 WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED

The design-to-ship cycle lost a step. You design on a canvas, agent builds it, it's live. No export. No rebuild. No "send to dev."

🧩 WHAT'S IN IT

① Ambient Intelligence — suggests design variations before you ask. Shows you where your design could go at every step.

② Mobbin built-in — 600K+ real screens from 1,000+ apps. Reference library is now a generation input. No account needed.

③ Design systems — upload yours or build from scratch. Every screen snaps to brand. Constraint architecture = built in.

④ Model choice — Claude, GPT-5, Gemini, Kimi, GLM. You pick the model for each session.

⑤ Canvas → Build — select any design frame, tell the agent to build it, it becomes a live running app.

💡 MY READ AS A SENIOR DESIGNER

The Ambient Intelligence framing is aspirational. "Ambient" suggestions can become noise fast if they're not well-tuned.

What I'm actually watching: do the design systems hold? If "everything snaps to brand in one click" is real, it's the first workflow-native constraint architecture in a no-code tool.

🎯 REAL HEADLINE

Not "Replit added design."
It's "design and shipping are now the same step."

When there's nothing to hand off, what does your handoff process become?
