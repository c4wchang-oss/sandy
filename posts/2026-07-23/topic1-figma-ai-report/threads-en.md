# Threads Post — Figma 2026 AI Report (English)
**Platform:** Threads
**Date:** July 23, 2026

---

Figma dropped their 2026 AI report. 8,403 responses, 10 countries, three years of longitudinal data.

The stat getting all the attention: 41% of designers now work in code.

That's not the headline.

The headline is that **the role boundary between design and engineering has dissolved**. 60% of developers are doing design work. 50%+ of teams abandoned linear handoffs. 61% are building interactive prototypes — that was 10% a year ago.

Three years ago, 7% of respondents said AI meaningfully changed how their team collaborates. Today: 41%. That's not gradual adoption — that's a phase transition.

The most important line in the report:

> "When AI can 10x anyone, personal productivity stops being the thing to optimize for."

I've been saying a version of this for months. Senior designers who are still optimizing for individual output velocity are solving the wrong problem. The new value is in orchestration — knowing what good looks like, directing the system, catching the wrong turns before they compound.

Two more data points worth sitting with:

76% of product builders now do at least half their work on the canvas. 1 in 5 developers would rather start a project there. The canvas isn't a design tool anymore. It's the team's shared operating surface.

And: 90% say design is at least as important as before AI. 60% say it's more important. 65% of developers say the same.

The interpretation: when execution gets cheaper, judgment gets more expensive. The bar for craft didn't drop — it shifted upward in abstraction. You're not designing pixels, you're designing decisions.

The design role didn't shrink. The design skillset got distributed.
