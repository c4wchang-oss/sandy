**Title:** Figma's 2026 AI Report: You're Focused on the Wrong Number

---

🔑 Everyone's talking about "41% of designers work in code now."

After 10+ years in UX, I think that's the wrong number to obsess over.

---

📊 Here's what actually matters from Figma's 2026 report (8,403 responses, 10 countries):

✅ 50%+ of product teams abandoned linear handoffs entirely
✅ 60% of engineers are now doing design work
✅ 61% build interactive prototypes (was 10% last year!)
✅ 70% say AI helped them do work they previously couldn't
✅ AI changed team collaboration: 7% → 41% in 3 years
✅ 76% of product builders do at least half their work on the canvas

---

🧠 The most important sentence in the entire report:

> "When AI can 10x anyone, personal productivity stops being the thing to optimize for."

Translation: individual speed is no longer your competitive edge.

What is? **Orchestration quality.**

Knowing what good looks like. Directing the system. Catching the wrong turns before they compound.

---

💡 This isn't about "designers should code"

The role boundary between design and engineering has dissolved. Engineers design on the canvas. Designers make judgment calls in the code layer. The whole team collaborates on the same surface.

90% of respondents say design is as important or more important post-AI.
65% of engineers say the same.

---

🎯 Senior designer interpretation:

The design role didn't shrink. The design skillset got distributed.

Your job is no longer just "the person who can design." It's "the person who can direct, judge, and elevate AI output."

The execution floor got lower.
The judgment ceiling got higher.

You're not designing pixels. You're designing decisions.
