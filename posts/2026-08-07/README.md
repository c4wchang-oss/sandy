### Topic 1: Adobe for ChatGPT — Unified Plugin (70+ Creative Tools)
**Source:** Adobe blog, Aug 6, 2026 — "Introducing Adobe for ChatGPT"
**Angle:** The quality ceiling isn't the plugin — it's your Creative Cloud library. AI output quality = the quality of the context you give it. Before adding any new AI workflow, ask whether your existing assets can actually support it.
**Key insight:** Adobe's "progressive disclosure of control" UX model (natural language → fine controls → desktop precision) is a thoughtful interaction decision; but organizing CC assets is now prerequisite infrastructure for AI-capable creative workflows.

### Topic 2: Figma Q2 2026 Earnings — AI Adoption Becomes Operational Infrastructure
**Source:** Figma Q2 2026 Earnings report, Aug 6, 2026 — investor.figma.com
**Angle:** When 80%+ of enterprise design orgs are using AI weekly, AI fluency stops being a differentiator and becomes a baseline. **Key data points:** 80%+ of paid customers above $10k ARR using AI credits weekly; 50%+ using Figma agent weekly; generative plugin creation doubled post-launch.
