**Title:**
AI can now build your onboarding flow. Here's the catch.

---

**Body:**

Usertour just shipped an MCP server with 67 tools, letting AI agents (Claude, Cursor) build, publish, and debug in-app onboarding flows directly.

As a UX designer, here's what this actually means for your workflow:

**What changed**
✦ AI agents can now write full onboarding flows end-to-end
✦ Validation is built in — broken structure gets caught before shipping
✦ Integration: Claude Code or Cursor + Usertour = onboarding without touching the UI manually

**The catch Usertour flagged in their own docs:**
"Ask for one focused, production-ready experience rather than a generic tour of every button."

"A tour of every button" = the AI default when you give it a vague brief. Fast, structurally clean, teaches nothing.

**What this means for your role**

📌 Step 1: Define what "success" looks like for a new user in their first 5 minutes
📌 Step 2: Identify which 2–3 actions they need to complete to get there
📌 Step 3: Specify what to show, what to defer, and what to skip entirely
📌 Step 4: Hand that to the AI — the 67 tools handle the build

**The senior designer skill that matters here:** knowing what users actually need to understand — and in what order. AI can structure, format, and ship. It can't prioritize for you.

**Honest take:** This doesn't replace UX thinking. It just makes a weak brief ship faster. If your brief was always the weak link, now you know.
