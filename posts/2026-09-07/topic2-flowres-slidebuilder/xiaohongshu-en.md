**Title:** The qual research reporting problem AI tools keep skipping — and what finally fixes it

---

📋 **What launched:**
flowres.io shipped SlideBuilder: a PowerPoint add-in that brings your analyzed research evidence directly into your deck — without tab-switching, copy-pasting, or rebuilding from scratch.

📌 **The problem it actually solves:**
Not "how do I make slides faster?"

The real bottleneck: your analysis is done in one tool, your deliverable has to be built in a completely different one. The transfer between them is manual rebuild work. Every time.

**Two types of AI presentation tools — know the difference:**

① **"Prompt to PPT"** (Gamma, Beautiful.ai, Claude Design)
→ AI generates slides from your description
→ Great for: general presentations, decks, content from scratch
→ Not great for: deliverables where the evidence has to be traceable

② **"Data to editable PPT"** (SlideBuilder)
→ AI scaffolds from evidence you've already curated and analyzed
→ Great for: qual research deliverables, client reports, findings decks
→ The evidence trail follows the slide into the deck

**How SlideBuilder works:**
1. Do your analysis in flowres.io
2. Bookmark themes, quotes, and summaries that belong in the report
3. Open PowerPoint → launch the flowres.io add-in
4. Browse your bookmarked evidence inside PowerPoint
5. Select what goes in → generate into your existing template
6. Edit like any native PowerPoint element

✅ **What's genuinely good:**
- Template fidelity preserved (important for client reports)
- Researcher decides what goes in — not AI guessing from text
- Evidence trail stays connected
- Works in your existing PowerPoint, not a new tool
- Free trial available

⚠️ **Honest limitations:**
- PowerPoint only (Google Slides not supported yet)
- Requires flowres.io account + completed analysis
- US, UK, Europe only currently
- Quality ceiling = how well you curated your bookmarks

💡 **Senior UX/Research take:**

SlideBuilder respects where researcher judgment belongs: shaping the narrative, choosing which evidence matters, deciding how the story flows.

It automates the part that doesn't require judgment: the physical transfer between tools.

That's a better division of labor than "AI writes your findings for you."

**Who this is for:**
→ UX researchers doing qual studies with client-facing deliverables
→ Research teams running multiple rounds per quarter
→ Anyone who's spent a Sunday rebuilding analysis into slides
