**Title:** ChatGPT now picks your response format — here's what that means for UX

**Body:**

GPT-6 Intelligent UI just rolled out to everyone today. Instead of always returning text, ChatGPT now generates the interface it thinks best fits your question. The model picks the format. You don't.

**What it generates:**
1. Interactive diagrams with tappable parts (ask how a bicycle works → labeled anatomy with clickable components)
2. Live adjustable widgets (recipe planning → change guest count, quantities update automatically)
3. Inline tools (split a bill → calculator appears in the conversation)
4. Side-by-side comparisons, charts, checklists
5. Plain text when that's genuinely cleaner

**The UX problem nobody's talking about:**
This is the "AI making IA decisions" pattern reaching the conversation layer.

- ChatGPT Visualize (Aug): AI picks your chart type from data
- Basedash (Oct 1): AI structures your whole dashboard IA from one sentence
- GPT-6 Intelligent UI: AI picks your response format from your question

At every scale, the failure mode is identical: confident, polished, wrong IA.

**What designers should actually test:**
When ChatGPT picks the wrong format — not confusingly wrong, but well-produced and wrong — do users push back? Or do they reorganize their understanding to match what was generated?

Nobody has rigorous data on this yet. That's the real design research gap.

**How our role shifts:**
- Before: we compose the interface
- Now: we evaluate whether the AI-generated interface serves the real user goal
- New skill: recognizing AI-composed misdirection vs. user adapting to it

**Which plans get what:**
- Plus / Pro / Business / Enterprise → GPT-6 Sol (richer model)
- Free / Go → GPT-6 Luna (more efficient model)
- No setting to override the format — the model decides
- Chat tab only; Work and Codex not affected

The frame isn't "chatbots got prettier." Format decision-making — one of the most fundamental UX choices — just moved to inference time.
