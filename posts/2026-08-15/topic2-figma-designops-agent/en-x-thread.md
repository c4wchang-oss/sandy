# X Thread — Figma AI Agent as Design System Maintainer (EN)
# Topic: Real-world testing of Figma AI agent confirms audit/maintenance strength, creation gaps (Aug 2026)
# Platform: X (Twitter)
# Format: 8-tweet thread

---

**Tweet 1 — Hook**
Months of real-world testing with Figma's AI agent has a clear conclusion.

It's not the design system builder people hoped for.

It's something more useful right now: a design system janitor.

Here's what that means for your role. 🧵

---

**Tweet 2**
What it does well:
- Audit variables that break naming conventions
- Flag detached components and hardcoded values
- Document components at scale
- Gap analysis across the system
- Consistency checks before publishing

---

**Tweet 3**
What it struggles with:

Creating new components that faithfully follow your established patterns.

Not because the model is bad.

Because "faithful to your system" requires context only you've built over time.

---

**Tweet 4**
This split matters.

AI handles structured, repeatable analytical work reliably.

AI inventing within a system's constraints — without deep context — produces drift.

The former scales. The latter needs supervision.

---

**Tweet 5**
The implication for senior designers:

Your job isn't maintenance anymore.

Your job is building a system *legible enough for AI to maintain.*

Naming, documentation, auto layout, component properties — these are now your AI context parameters.

---

**Tweet 6**
What "legible enough" means in practice:

- Variables with semantic names (not "Blue-300")
- Component descriptions that explain when to use, not just what it looks like
- Auto layout on everything — it's how agents understand spatial intent
- Component properties that define every state

---

**Tweet 7**
A practical review-tier framework:

Low risk: copy, spacing, minor visual changes → AI reviews, no approval needed
High risk: new components, checkout flows, accessibility-sensitive patterns → human sign-off required

Design the gates. Don't police every pixel.

---

**Tweet 8 — CTA**
The design system isn't just a library anymore.

It's the context window your AI agent operates inside.

Your publishing standards and your AI quality standards just became the same document.
