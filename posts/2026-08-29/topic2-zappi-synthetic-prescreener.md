## Threads Post (English)

Research has always been most useful at the front of the ideation process. It's also always been most expensive there.

So teams defaulted to doing it late. Pick 5 "safe" ideas first. Commission research after the pool is narrow. Call it prioritization.

Zappi launched a Synthetic Idea Pre-Screener this week (Aug 26). Upload up to 300 early concepts — text, images, or both. Get back a trial rank, distinctiveness flags, and consumer-response themes in about 15 minutes. No recruitment. No quotas.

The mechanics: synthetic respondents built on real demographic and behavioral profiles, validated against hundreds of live human studies. 88% of concepts ranked in the top 30% by the Pre-Screener also performed high or medium with real consumers.

That's a directional filter, not a prediction. There's a difference — and it matters.

The design implication I'm watching: the ideation funnel is about to change shape.

The funnel itself stays the same — you still narrow down and invest deeper research in fewer concepts. But the cost structure changed. You no longer have to pre-filter based on internal judgment before you get any signal. Cast wide. Pre-screen fast. Then decide what deserves human research budget.

That means you don't have to play it safe early to justify a research investment. That changes what bold ideation looks like when it's backed by an org that has to justify creative bets.

The honest caveats: FMCG and QSR categories only in beta. US + UK customers. The quality of your synthetic signal scales with the quality of your concept definitions — garbage stimuli in, garbage ranking out.

But the direction is clear. Research used to gate ideation. Now it can open it.
