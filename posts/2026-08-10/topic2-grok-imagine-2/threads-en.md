Grok Imagine Image 2.0 landed with a #2 Arena score in image editing. The only model ahead is GPT-image-2.

What's interesting to me isn't the ranking. It's what "precision editing" means for product design work.

Magic Wand (click a specific region, edit just that, leave the rest untouched), segmentation selection, and background removal with transparent PNG output — these are the exact operations that used to require Photoshop for AI-generated mockup work.

The practical implication: the "hero image for a feature spec" used to be a half-day task. Generate in Midjourney, edit in Photoshop, export for the research session, re-edit when the brief changes. Now it's a short conversation with precision refinements.

I've been testing this for UX research stimulus material. Sketch rough scenario → generate base in Grok Imagine → Magic Wand to fix product placement → export for study. Tasks that used to take hours are getting closer to 20 minutes.

Honest caveat though: Arena rankings measure "looks good" not "matches specification." For strict brand accuracy or proportional constraints, test it in your actual workflow before committing to it. The frontier labs are all within striking distance of each other now — 4+ models at competitive quality. Don't build your workflow around one model's current benchmark position.

The larger read: we've crossed the threshold where the bottleneck in AI image work is no longer the tool. It's the brief. What image should exist, where, for whom, telling what story — that's now the entire game. The senior designer value isn't in knowing how to edit; it's in knowing what to make.
