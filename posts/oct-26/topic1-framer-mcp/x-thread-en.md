**Tweet 7**
Two limits worth knowing before you start:

1. No localization at launch. Teams with i18n workflows: stay on local CLI for now.

2. Explicit prompts only. Name the page, the section, the component. The agent sees less canvas context than Framer's own built-in tool. Vague = unreliable.
