**Tweet 3**
Why LoRA matters for brand production:

A brand prompt is a description of what you want.
A LoRA is a trained parameter of what your brand actually looks like.

One produces approximations.
The other produces your specific product in every scene you generate.

---

**Tweet 4**
Regional editing solves a different problem:

Your campaign image is 90% right. The background doesn't work.

Before: regenerate everything. Accept new errors in the parts that were fine.

Krea 3: select only the background region. Describe the new scene. Everything else is preserved exactly.

**Tweet 5**
Production workflow implication:

Train one LoRA per product or visual identity.
Edit regionally for campaign variations.
Generate at 2K in 8 seconds.

The bottleneck moves from "waiting for image generation" to "writing a precise regional edit description."

**Tweet 6**
Brief quality is now training quality:

A LoRA trained on 4 blurry product photos → inconsistent results at scale.
A LoRA trained on 40 high-quality, consistent lighting shots → brand-accurate results every time.

The brief is no longer text you type.
It's the images you upload to train.
