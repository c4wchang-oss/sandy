# 小紅書 Post — Adobe as AI Platform (English)
**Topic:** Adobe expands Creative Cloud to Gemini + interactive editing in Claude (Sep 24, 2026)

---

**Title:**
Adobe is inside every AI assistant now — is your Creative Cloud actually ready?

---

**Body:**

Adobe just landed in Google Gemini this week. And inside Claude, there's now interactive editing for PDFs and Express designs. 80+ Creative Cloud tools callable from five AI assistants: ChatGPT, Claude, Slack, Copilot, Gemini.

The announcements were full of platform counts and tool numbers. Here's what actually matters for designers and creative teams:

**① The model flipped.**
AI used to live inside Adobe (Firefly in Photoshop). Now Adobe lives inside AI assistants. Creative Cloud is the plugin, not the platform. That's not a feature update — it's a structural shift in where creative work happens.

**② The interactive editor in Claude is the real UX story.**
Two modes: describe what you want → AI does it. Or: step into the PDF/Express design and edit it yourself — reorder pages, edit text, adjust colors, right inside the conversation. First AI plugin I've seen apply progressive disclosure at the interface layer, not just the feature layer. The in-context editor resolves the trust gap between "AI did it" and "I trust it enough not to check."

**③ Quality ceiling = your CC library. Not the model.**
Firefly output quality, Express template selection, asset organization — that's still the ceiling. 80 tools just creates 5x more places where that same ceiling shows up. Clean up your library before these tools start calling it.

**④ New governance question for every creative team.**
Your brand assets are now execution targets in five separate AI conversations simultaneously. Who decides what gets generated, where? Who catches when the AI's interpretation of "on-brand" starts diverging between Claude and Gemini? Nobody's designed an answer yet.

**⑤ Don't optimize for tool count. Optimize for library quality.**
80 tools means nothing if the CC library it executes from is disorganized. The quality ceiling hasn't moved. The surface area where it matters has multiplied by five.

---

**Honest caveat:**
80 tools across 5 platforms with different auth models, rate limits, and context windows will have rough edges and inconsistencies. Expect some tools to behave differently across platforms. Plan for it.

**Reframe:**
Adobe didn't just add features. It repositioned Creative Cloud as AI execution infrastructure. The question is whether your library is organized like infrastructure — or like a junk drawer.
