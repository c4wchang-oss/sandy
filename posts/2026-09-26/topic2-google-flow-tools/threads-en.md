# Threads Post — Google Flow Six Creator-Built Tools (English)
**Topic:** Google Flow releases 6 specialized AI workflow tools co-designed with domain experts (Sep 23, 2026)

---

## Long-form reflection

Six new tools in Google Flow this week. Sound design, architectural material ideation, caption animation, Swiss-style motion graphics. Co-designed with people who actually work in those fields.

I've spent time with the announcements and the thing that keeps standing out isn't any individual tool. It's the authorship model behind them.

Google didn't build generalist creative AI and hope professionals would figure out how to use it. They found a sound designer, an interior designer, a filmmaker, and asked a specific question: what's the part of your workflow that's tedious and structured enough to automate? Then they built that specific thing.

SwissFlow Studio is a good example. Converts scripts into Swiss-style motion graphics. "Swiss-style" isn't a vibe description — it's a design system. Specific typographic rules, grid logic, compositional principles with a long tradition behind them. The constraint is encoded into the tool. You're not prompting for a style and hoping the AI interprets it correctly. The style IS the brief.

Mondo Sónico outputs editable audio stems. Not one flat file. That's a production-specific decision. The output is designed to fit into a professional post-production workflow — not to replace it. The AI result is the starting point, not the artifact.

Honest caveat: we don't have data on whether these tools actually save time in real production contexts. The premise is "removes tedious steps without limiting editing flexibility." That's testable. We haven't seen the test results yet.

For design teams: the question worth sitting with is which part of my production pipeline is tedious-but-structured? Not the judgment calls. Not the taste decisions. The parts where you know what the output should look like — you just have to produce it manually every time. Figuring out that answer is a design exercise, not a technology exercise.

## Short punchy take (post separately)

Most AI tools: build something general, hope professionals adopt it.

Google Flow's model: find the person who knows the tedious part. Build that specific thing.

The domain expert doesn't just use the workflow.
The domain expert architects it.

That's a different job description.
One worth adding to yours.
