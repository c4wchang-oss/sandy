# X Thread — EN
## Topic: Framer Agents July 2026 Update — Accessibility as Workflow
## Date: Aug 4, 2026

---

**Tweet 1/8**
Framer just made accessibility part of the agent's job description.

Not a checklist. Not a post-launch audit. A conversation you have while building the thing.

---

**Tweet 2/8**
The July 2026 Agents update added support for A11Y layer attributes.

You can now tell the Framer agent: "run an accessibility pass on my navigation and hero CTAs" — and it applies alt text, labels on icon-only buttons, and ARIA metadata across all of them.

Not one by one. All of it.

---

**Tweet 3/8**
Page Effects also landed in Agent scope.

Scroll-linked animations, entrance transitions, full-page motion — all of it is now promptable. Point at a section, describe the behavior, the agent builds it.

This matters because motion and accessibility used to be the two things most likely to get "we'll fix it later."

---

**Tweet 4/8**
The design industry has been saying "accessibility is not a feature, it's a foundation" for 20 years.

That sentence is finally true at the workflow level. When the agent can do an accessibility pass in the same conversation you're building the page, the excuse of "not enough time" starts to sound different.

---

**Tweet 5/8**
What it actually looks like in practice:

You build a homepage with Framer Agents. Then in the same chat: "check all form fields for proper labels and missing alt text."

The agent scans, applies, reports back. You're not exporting to an audit tool. The audit is in the workflow.

---

**Tweet 6/8**
Other things in the update worth noting:
– Richer Overlays: Enter/Exit backdrop transitions, safe areas for floating overlays
– CMS Rich Text Video: full CMS video now in Agent scope
– Text Shadows: finally AI-editable
– Draggable interactions: prompted, not hand-coded

The list reads like everything that used to require manual layer edits is now in the conversation layer.

**Tweet 8/8**
Real headline isn't "Framer Agents got smarter."

The excuse to ship them later just got harder to make.
