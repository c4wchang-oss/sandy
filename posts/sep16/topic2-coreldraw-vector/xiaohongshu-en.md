**Title:** CorelDRAW now generates editable vectors from text prompts — and the style selector is the actual design

---

CorelDRAW's September 2026 update just shipped Text-to-Vector AI. Here's what actually matters.

**What it does:**
→ Enter a text prompt or reference artwork in the AI Generate panel
→ Choose a style: Line art / Comic book / Icon / Hand drawn / Flat illustration / Kawaii pastel
→ Get editable, scalable vector art in seconds
→ Refine nodes, colors, shapes in CorelDRAW's existing tools
→ Output respects your document's color mode

**What makes the style selector worth paying attention to:**
It's not a convenience feature. It's constraint architecture baked into the product. They bounded the generation space so you can't generate "nothing." You pick a category, then describe within it.

This is the right product design approach. Free-form vector generation fails because users need to pick a direction before they can prompt well.

---

**The PowerTRACE update is actually more useful for most production teams:**
→ 4× faster bitmap-to-vector conversion
→ AI background removal
→ Better structure + color handling
→ Cleaner output from client-supplied raster artwork

---

**For UX designers specifically:**
→ Good for quick icon ideation in prototyping phases
→ Useful for placeholder visual assets (no licensing risk)
→ Not a design system asset generator yet — but the direction is right

---

**Honest check:**
Every major design tool has this now. Figma, Adobe, Canva — all have AI vector/image generation. CorelDRAW is reaching parity, not leading. Differentiation now is about workflow integration, not generation capability.

**Free unlimited generation Sep 15–22 for all CorelDRAW users (trial and paid).**

---

**What didn't change:** knowing what vector the brand needs. That's still your job.
