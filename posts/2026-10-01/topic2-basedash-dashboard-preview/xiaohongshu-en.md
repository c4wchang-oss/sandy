**Title:** AI just started making IA decisions for your data products

---

Basedash launched something today (Oct 1): ask for a dashboard in chat, and AI builds the whole thing — live and interactive — right there in the conversation.

You type one sentence:
"Build a sales dashboard with pipeline, win rate, and revenue by rep."

The AI automatically decides:
→ Which KPIs sit at the top
→ What gets a chart vs a leaderboard table
→ How many tabs, what goes in each

These aren't layout choices. They're Information Architecture choices.

---

**What they got right ✅**

The preview renders the real dashboard — not a mockup. You can switch date ranges, click tabs, and filter right there in the conversation. "Open dashboard" just takes you to the same thing at full size.

No gap between preview and production. This should be the standard for agentic tools.

---

**But here's the UX point worth naming ⚠️**

The decisions AI makes — which metrics to prioritize, what format to use, how to categorize — those are IA decisions.

The question isn't whether AI can do this. It's:
Are those IA decisions based on the question your actual users are trying to answer?

Is this dashboard for a VP scanning for signal or an IC tracking daily pipeline? Same prompt. Completely different correct IA.

AI doesn't know. Only you do.

---

**The bottleneck shifted**

Before: "How do we build this dashboard?"
After: "Is this the right IA for the question we're actually trying to answer?"

Senior UX value: not building the dashboard — evaluating whether the IA is right.

---

Best used when the question is already sharp. Use it before the question is clear and you get a polished, confident, wrong IA.

Impressive feature? Yes. Does the core problem disappear? No — it just moved up a level.

Anyone using Basedash? How are you handling AI-generated IA quality? 👇
