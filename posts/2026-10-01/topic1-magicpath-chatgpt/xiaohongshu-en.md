**Title:** ChatGPT now has a design canvas — here's what's actually different

---

MagicPath launched a ChatGPT plugin last week. You get an infinite design canvas right next to the conversation window — from idea to editable UI, all in one place.

A few design decisions in this that I think are worth paying attention to 👇

**1. Select = Context (no more text descriptions)**
Whatever you select on the canvas becomes context for your next message.

Before: "I mean the card on the left, the one with rounded corners, with the user photo at the top, that kind of layout..."

Now: Select it.

**2. Parallel agents (genuinely concurrent)**
Ask for 3 directions and it dispatches 3 concurrent designer agents — one per screen — running simultaneously. Three directions appear as each finishes. Real parallel exploration, not sequential turns.

**3. Drop in references directly**
Screenshots, moodboards, UI from your repo — drag onto the canvas, select it, and it becomes your visual brief. No text description of "the feel I'm going for."

**4. Output is runnable React code**
Export directly from the design and connect it to your existing codebase.

---

**Senior UX take:**

The direction is right — turning the canvas from "the delivery artifact at the end" into "the thinking layer throughout the conversation."

But this is a plugin integration, not a native design tool. You'll feel the seams. Output quality still depends on what reference you select and what direction you give.

**Good for:**
✅ Quick concept direction exploration
✅ Wireframe-level alignment discussions
✅ Early ideation where visual briefing matters

**Not ready for:**
❌ Production-ready design
❌ Precise design system alignment
