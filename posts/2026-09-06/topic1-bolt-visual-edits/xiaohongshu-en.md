**Title:** Bolt just added click-to-edit. As a UX designer, here's why it matters

---

🖱️ **What happened:**
Bolt.new launched Visual Edits on Sep 3.
You can now click any element in your running app → toolbar appears → adjust text, fonts, colors → stack changes → send in one batch.

📌 **What it actually solves:**
Not precision. The revision loop.

The AI app builder complaints I hear most from clients:
"Why do I have to describe everything in words?"
"Can't I just... click the thing I want to change?"

Now you can. (Sort of.)

✅ **What works well:**
- Direct text editing on canvas
- Font size, styling, color via toolbar
- Stack multiple edits → send once (fewer tokens, faster session)
- Available on free + paid plans

⚠️ **Honest limitations:**
- No drag-to-reorder layout
- No multi-element selection
- Text and styling only — no spatial editing

They were upfront about it: "scope is narrow by design."
That's actually responsible scoping.

💡 **Senior UX take:**
Direct manipulation is a 44-year-old principle (Shneiderman, 1982). Figma built their entire product around it. AI builders are finally catching up.

**How I'd use it:**
→ Prompts for layout-level decisions (structure, IA, flow)
→ Visual Edits for micro-refinement (size, color, copy tweaks)
→ Use it live in client sessions — they watch you click instead of waiting for regeneration

This is the revision workflow fix we needed.
Not perfect yet — spatial editing is still missing.
But the direction is right.
