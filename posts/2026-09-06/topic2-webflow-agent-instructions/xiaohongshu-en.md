**Title:** Webflow auto-generates your brand guidelines. Here's the catch.

---

📋 **What launched (Sep 2):**
Webflow Agent Instructions Generation
AI reads your live site → auto-writes:
- Brand Guidelines (voice, tone, personality)
- Design System Guide (components, variables, usage)
- Asset Guidelines (image/media rules)
- CMS Guidelines (collection + field-level rules)

Every agent editing your site uses these as context.
Sounds great. Right?

🤔 **The uncomfortable truth:**
It reads what's actually on your site.
Not what you intended.
Not your original design brief.

Every override a developer made at 11pm before a deadline.
Every legacy component nobody cleaned up.
Every inconsistency across 3 years of incremental updates.

That's now your "brand standard." And agents will follow it faithfully.

🔍 **How I'd actually use this:**

1⃣ Generate the instructions
2⃣ Read them like audit findings — what's wrong?
3⃣ Fix the worst site inconsistencies first
4⃣ Edit the instructions with actual intent
5⃣ THEN let agents follow them

Generate → Audit → Fix → Trust. Sequence matters.

⚠️ **The governance gap everyone's missing:**
"AI generated it from my site" ≠ "it's correct"

Who reviews these instructions before agents act on them?

That reviewer is now a designer role.

💡 **Senior UX take:**
This is accidentally the best design debt visualizer I've seen — not because it's smart, but because it's honest. It shows you exactly what AI infers about your brand from shipped work.

The gap between what shipped and what was meant? That's yours to close before AI inherits it.
