**Title:**
Figma Design Agent + Weave go GA on Oct 6 — what "variable credit cost" actually means, and the governance checklist to run before it starts

---

**Body:**

Figma's Design agent and Weave tools exit free beta on October 6 — four days from now. The free experimentation window is closing. Here's what you need to understand and do before the credit meter starts.

**What's changing:**

① Figma Design agent starts consuming AI credits on October 6
② Weave tools in Figma Design start consuming credits on October 6
③ Both draw from the same shared credit pool as Figma Make and other AI features

**Two different pricing models — and why it matters:**

**Weave tools = fixed credit cost per tool**
Predictable. Auditable. Same structure as Figma's image AI features. Good for governance.

**Design agent = variable credit cost** based on:
- Complexity of your prompt
- Number of actions the agent takes to complete the task
- Context you provide (connected libraries, attached files)

**The implication most teams will miss:**

A better brief costs more credits per session — but produces better output faster with fewer revision loops.

Brief quality is now also credit efficiency. These are the same optimization problem.

An underspecified prompt burns credits while the agent resolves your ambiguity. That's a double cost: worse output AND higher consumption.

**The Figma Workflow Lab tutorial (published Oct 1) is worth reading now:**

It shows the high-leverage Weave pattern — design system Figma nodes as locked constraints → generation → Any LLM nodes for localization across 5 markets with market-specific copy guidelines → editable Figma layers as output.

Fixed cost per tool. Predictable budget. This is where Weave's credit model works in your favor.

**Governance checklist — do this before October 6:**

✅ Identify who on your team has been using the Design agent in free beta, and for which tasks
✅ Decide which workflows are worth the credit cost, and which were exploratory
✅ Set per-user credit limits in Figma Admin (available since August) if not done yet
✅ Audit your connected libraries — their quality affects both output and credit consumption
✅ Check your team's credit allocation — Professional plan got 2x more credits at the same price on August 25

**The honest note:**

If no one on your team has been tracking Design agent usage during the free beta, you have no usage baseline to project costs from. That gap is worth addressing in the next four days — even informally.

**The senior UX takeaway:**

AI tool governance isn't IT or Finance. It's DesignOps. Figma put the credit management tools in the admin console. Someone on the design leadership side needs to own that console before October 6, not after.
