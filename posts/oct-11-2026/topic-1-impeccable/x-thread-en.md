**Tweet 3:**

→ Side-tab accent borders, gradient text on headings, purple gradients, bounce easing, dark mode glowing accents

"AI generated this" tells. And "nobody QA'd this" tells.

**Tweet 5:**
The thing I find most important: PRODUCT.md.

Impeccable distinguishes product truth from visual direction. PRODUCT.md records audience, purpose, operating context, constraints, voice, evidence.

That's different from DESIGN.md, GUI.md, Library Guidelines — those are about visual style.

Conflating product truth and visual direction is where most AI design briefs actually break down.
