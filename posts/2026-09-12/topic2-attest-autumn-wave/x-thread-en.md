**Tweet 1 (Hook)**
Your research isn't stuck because you ran bad studies.

It's stuck because the output is in a folder that nothing can read.

Attest just changed that.

🧵

---

**Tweet 2**
Attest's Autumn Wave (Sep 10, 2026) launched three things:

→ Results API: every completed study lands automatically in your data warehouse or BI tool
→ MCP server: query your entire study archive from Claude, ChatGPT, or any AI tool
→ Push-to-talk AI interviews: respondents get thinking time before they record

---

**Tweet 3**
The MCP server is the structural shift.

Before: research lives in Attest. You export a CSV when you need it.
Now: agents can query across everything you've ever run. Synthesis across waves, markets, time periods — in one answer.

Your research archive compounds in value every time you add to it.

---

**Tweet 4**
Quality ceiling is still your research question.

MCP makes your archive queryable. It doesn't improve what's in it.
Vague study objectives → vague synthesis.
Poorly framed questions → AI finds patterns in noise.

The bottleneck moved from "can we retrieve this?" to "was it worth asking?"

---

**Tweet 5**
The push-to-talk format addresses a real critique.

AI-moderated interviews were criticized for not giving respondents thinking time — pace was too fast, responses felt reactive.

New format: moderator reads question aloud → respondent presses to record → presses again when done.

Thinking time before recording. More considered answers.

---

**Tweet 6**
What the Results API means for research ops:

Before: export CSV → email to data engineer → wait for upload → analysis.
Now: set it up once → every completed study automatically lands in the warehouse.

Research data becomes a running asset. Not a series of one-off reports that go quiet the moment they're delivered.

---

**Tweet 7**
Honest caveats:

→ Results API and MCP server are in early access (enterprise customers first)
→ AI moderation captures patterns and themes, not hesitation, tone, and subtext a trained moderator would notice
→ Archive only compounds if studies were structured consistently — inconsistent framing breaks synthesis

---

**Tweet 8 (CTA)**
The senior researcher's role just got clearer:

You're not writing reports.
You're architecting a research library.

Question design, recruitment logic, cross-wave taxonomy, consistency — that's the work now.

Is your archive structured enough for an agent to synthesize from? ↓
