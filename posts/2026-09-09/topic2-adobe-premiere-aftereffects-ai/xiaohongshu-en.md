**Title:** Adobe IBC 2026: Two AI Updates for Premiere and After Effects — Worth Separating

---

🎯 **What happened:**
September 8, 2026 — Adobe released two AI updates at IBC 2026:
- **Premiere**: Generative Media Tool (generate video/SFX/music directly in timeline)
- **After Effects**: AI Assistant public beta (cross-project natural language operations)

Media coverage focused on "generate video in timeline."
That's the less interesting half.

📌 **Two tools solving different problems:**

**Premiere — Generative Media Tool (shipping)**
Draw a range on your timeline → describe what's needed → pick a model (Firefly, Veo, Kling, Runway, Luma) → generate it into that gap without leaving the app.

Five models to pick from. You're not picking which is "better."
You're selecting which aesthetic + technical profile fits this specific shot.

Worth noting: the model picker shows credit cost before you commit — financial transparency in a creative tool is still uncommon and matters.

**After Effects — AI Assistant (public beta)**
This is the more interesting one.

After Effects has accumulated ~30 years of features. The result: an extraordinarily capable application that's correspondingly good at hiding the thing you need three panels deep.

AI Assistant adds a navigation layer — not creative replacement.

✅ **What After Effects AI Assistant actually does:**

1. **Writes expressions (most significant)**
   Describe the rig you want in plain language → it writes JavaScript + builds adjustable sliders
   "Build a wiggle rig with controls for amount, frequency and decay" → done
   Expressions were AE's last "must learn to code" barrier. There's a door now.

2. **Organizes inherited complex projects**
   Inheriting a 500-layer AE project with no documentation?
   Assistant analyzes → proposes reorganization → executes after your approval
   Every action is undoable

3. **Works across the entire project**
   Not just your open comp — the whole project
   Finds issues, fixes broken expressions, inspects all footage

4. **Transparent operation**
   Shows tool calls, explains reasoning, asks clarifying questions before acting
   The interaction model I trust: transparent + undoable + show-your-work

⚠️ **Honest caveats:**
→ Generate Video + SFX are shipping. Music and Soundscape are still beta.
→ Video generation costs credits — they stack up fast, billing surprise at month end
→ After Effects AI Assistant is public beta, not GA
→ Free: audio cleanup, Color Mode, AI Assistant tasks
→ Paid credits: video generation, SFX generation

💡 **Senior designer perspective:**

What motion designers keep doing: aesthetic judgment on generated clips, model selection for specific shots, deciding what the animation should feel like and why

What gets automated: expression debugging, menu archaeology on inherited projects, reorganizing someone else's complex comp architecture

The administrative overhead was always what deserved to be automated first.

**The model picker pattern (3rd time this year):**
Figma August 3 → Divi September 5 → Premiere September 8
Signal: model literacy is becoming a baseline creative skill.
Which model you choose = your aesthetic direction decision.
