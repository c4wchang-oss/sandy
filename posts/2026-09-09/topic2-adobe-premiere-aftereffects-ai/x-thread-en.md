**Tweet 1 (Hook)**
Adobe shipped Premiere's Generative Media Tool and After Effects AI Assistant this week at IBC.

The media coverage focused on "generate video in timeline."

That's the less interesting half of the story.

🧵

---

**Tweet 2**
What shipped (September 8, IBC 2026):

Premiere → Generative Media Tool:
→ Draw a range on timeline → describe what's needed → generate in-place
→ Model picker: Firefly, Veo, Kling, Runway, Luma
→ Credit cost shown before you generate

After Effects → AI Assistant (public beta):
→ Plain language → native AE operations across whole project
→ Writes expression rigs from natural language descriptions
→ Works across entire project, not just the open comp
→ Every action is undoable

---

**Tweet 3**
After Effects has accumulated ~30 years of capabilities.

The result: an application that's extraordinarily capable and correspondingly good at hiding the command you need three panels deep.

The AI Assistant isn't replacing creative judgment.
It's adding a navigation layer over accumulated complexity.

That's exactly what it should do.

---

**Tweet 4**
The expression writing is the most significant part for motion designers.

Expressions are the closest thing After Effects has to code. Powerful, and a hard barrier — you either learned JavaScript or stayed in keyframe-only territory.

Plain language → expression rig with adjustable sliders.

The last "must learn to code" wall in AE just got a door in it.

---

**Tweet 5**
The model picker in Premiere is a pattern we've seen three times this year:
→ Figma model picker (August 3)
→ Divi AI models (September 5)
→ Premiere Generative Media Tool (September 8)

You're not picking which model is "better."
You're selecting which aesthetic and technical profile fits this specific shot.

Model literacy = creative direction. That's a skill now.

---

**Tweet 6**
Honest caveats:

→ Generate Video + SFX are shipping. Music and Soundscape are still beta.
→ Video generation costs credits. The billing surprise shows up at month end.
→ After Effects AI Assistant is public beta. Not GA.
→ "Shows tool calls and explains reasoning" is noted. Transparent, undoable interaction model. That's the right architecture.

---

**Tweet 7**
What changes for senior motion designers:

You stop spending time on:
→ Expression debugging
→ Menu archaeology on inherited projects
→ Reorganizing someone else's 500-layer comp

You keep doing:
→ Aesthetic judgment on generated clips
→ Model selection for the shot
→ Deciding what the motion should feel like

The administrative overhead was always what deserved to be automated.
