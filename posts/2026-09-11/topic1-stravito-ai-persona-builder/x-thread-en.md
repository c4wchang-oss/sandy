**Tweet 2**
Stravito AI Persona Builder launched Sep 9.

You attach your company's own research — studies, interviews, transcripts, collections.
Two agents run: one drafts an evidence-cited profile, one structures it into trait cards.

Every trait is scored, linked to its exact source, and flagged as contested if sources disagree.

---

**Tweet 3**
The "Glass Box" principle is what actually matters here.

Where research doesn't cover a topic, the persona says so.
It doesn't invent an answer.

That's a design decision — not default language model behavior.

Most AI tools do the opposite.

---

**Tweet 4**
Here's the quality ceiling:

Your research corpus.

Not the model.
Not the platform.
Your research.

→ Thin research → thin personas
→ Contested findings in corpus → contested traits flagged, not silently averaged
→ Recency tracked: recent research outweighs old

**Tweet 5**
