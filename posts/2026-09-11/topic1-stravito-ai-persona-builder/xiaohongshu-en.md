**Title:** The AI Persona Trust Problem Has an Answer — And It's About Citations, Not Capability

---

🎯 **What happened:**
Stravito launched AI Persona Builder on Sep 9, 2026. It builds AI personas from your company's own research — studies, interviews, transcripts, collections. Every trait is scored, linked to its exact source, and flagged as contested if your research contradicts itself.

Where research doesn't cover a topic, the persona says so instead of inventing an answer.

This is Glass Box AI: you can see the research behind every answer, how strongly it's backed, and where gaps exist. Most AI persona tools are the opposite.

📌 **How it works:**

1. **Define** who the persona represents + attach your research documents
2. **Generate** — two specialized agents (one drafts evidence-cited profile, one structures trait cards)
3. **Review + Refine** — see every trait's source before publishing
4. **Interview** — anyone on the team can query the persona; source citations visible with every response

📌 **Why this matters for UX designers:**

1. **The quality ceiling is your research corpus**
   Thin research → thin personas.
   Contested findings in archive → contested traits flagged (not silently smoothed over).
   You can't put a persona on a slide and say "we validated this" unless what's behind it is solid.

2. **Source traceability changes the review conversation**
   "Why does the persona say this?" now has a page number.
   That changes who can challenge it and how — including stakeholders who disagree.

3. **The work shifts from creation to curation**
   Generation is fast. Maintaining a research archive clean enough for AI to build from — that's the ongoing role.

✅ **What to do with this:**

1. **Audit your existing research archive**
   Is it centralized? Searchable? Tagged by topic? Recent enough?
   This is now a quality-input question, not just an information architecture question.

2. **Document research recency explicitly**
   Stravito weights recent research more heavily. Know which of your studies still reflect current users.

3. **Treat contested traits as research design signals**
   If the persona flags a trait as contested, your research is showing you something inconsistent. That's worth investigating, not resolving by picking a side.

4. **Identify decisions that still need real users**
   AI personas are synthesis tools. They reflect what you gave them. Discovery research — finding what you didn't know to ask — still needs real people.

⚠️ **Honest caveats:**
Q4 2026 GA target (early access only now). Source traceability makes bias auditable, not absent — if your research has sampling bias, the persona inherits it. Primary audience is marketing/insights teams, not product design teams. Not a replacement for talking to actual users.

💡 **The reframe:**
The problem was never "can AI make a persona quickly?"
The problem was always "where did this trait come from, and should I trust it?"
Stravito built the audit trail.
Your research quality is still the answer.
