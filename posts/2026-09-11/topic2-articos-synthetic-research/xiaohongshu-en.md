**Title:** Synthetic User Research Finally Made an Auditable Claim — Here's What 86% Actually Means

---

🎯 **What launched:**
Articos launched its peer-reviewed synthetic user research platform on Sep 10, 2026, with Live Calls — real-time voice conversations with AI-generated personas — now generally available.

Key claim: 86% theme recall against Nielsen Norman Group and Baymard Institute benchmarks. Validated across 46 studies in 9 research domains. This is a published methodology — not a marketing stat. Direct ChatGPT prompting for research scored 7.5x less accurately in the same test.

Plans start at $29 for 2 studies.

📌 **How it works:**

→ Input a brief (concept, landing page, messaging variant)
→ Articos generates 12–50 synthetic personas using Big Five personality traits + domain context
→ Hypothesis-blind interviews: each persona answers independently without knowing your hypothesis
→ Adversarial review: skeptics and blockers included in every panel by default
→ Evidence chains: every finding links to a specific persona quote with traceable citation
→ Full report in under 30 minutes

**Live Calls** (now GA): real-time voice conversations with simulated personas for follow-up probing.

📌 **What "86% theme recall" actually means:**

✓ Articos surfaces 86% of themes that expert human researchers found
✓ Methodology published and auditable (46 studies, 9 domains, NNG/Baymard benchmarks)
✓ Structured research protocol beats raw AI prompting (7.5x more accurate than ChatGPT)

✗ Not 86% accuracy on emotional nuance
✗ Not 86% at discovering unknown unknowns
✗ Per-domain scores vary — not all research types perform equally
✗ Does not replace moderated interviews for high-stakes decisions

📌 **Right use cases vs. wrong ones:**

✅ **Use Articos for:**
- Concept validation before recruiting real participants ($$$)
- Message testing across multiple copy variants at scale
- Directional signals: is this problem real enough to pursue?
- Refining your research questions before running a full study
- Early-stage brief development when you don't know what to probe

❌ **Don't use Articos for:**
- Discovery research (finding what you didn't know to ask)
- High-stakes UX decisions with real user consequences
- Emotional nuance, cultural context, behavioral edge cases
- Final validation before shipping

✅ **What to do with this:**

1. **Build a triage protocol for your team**
   Which decisions get synthetic research first? Which go straight to moderated sessions?
   Document it. Not every question needs a $5,000 study.

2. **Invest more in research question design**
   Articos' quality ceiling is your question, not the platform.
   Vague question → vague findings — same as with human research.

3. **Use Articos round 1 to sharpen round 2**
   Run synthetic research. Identify the themes that need verification.
   Let that shape your screener, guide questions, and focus areas for live moderated sessions.

4. **Use Live Calls for probing, not opening**
   Voice conversations are most useful after you already have a finding to probe deeper.
   Not ideal as your opening research instrument.

5. **Stay honest about what you got**
   When presenting findings from synthetic research, say so.
   "Articos surfaced this pattern across 30 AI personas" is a different claim than "users told us."

⚠️ **Honest caveats:**
Early-stage company (founded 2025). 86% is theme recall across 9 domains — per-domain results vary, methodology is published so you can check. Best used alongside real user research, not as a replacement. Starts at $29 for 2 studies with a 7-day free trial.

Synthetic research didn't take the researcher's job.
It moved the bottleneck.
The expensive part used to be recruitment and scheduling.
Now it's having the right research question in the first place.
The researcher who writes the clearest brief gets the most out of Articos — same as any other tool.
