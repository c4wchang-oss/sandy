---

86% theme recall against Nielsen Norman Group and Baymard benchmarks.

That's the number Articos launched with this week, alongside Live Calls — real-time voice conversations with AI-generated research personas.

It's the first number I've seen in the synthetic research space that I can actually trace. They published their methodology: 46 studies across 9 domains, compared against published expert findings from NNG and Baymard. Every theme each platform surfaced was scored against the other. 86% overlap. In the same test, prompting ChatGPT directly for research scored 7.5x less accurately. That gap isn't surprising — but having a number for it matters.

The methodology design is genuinely rigorous for synthetic research: hypothesis-blind interviews (each persona answers in isolation without knowing what you're trying to find), adversarial panels (skeptics and blockers included in every panel by default), and evidence chains that link every finding back to a specific persona quote. This is structured research protocol applied to AI-generated participants. Not just "ask the AI what users think."

Live Calls adds real-time voice. Written prompts don't simulate conversation dynamics. For follow-up probing — taking a finding and going deeper on it — that's a real methodological gap closed.

Honest read: 86% theme recall is not 86% accuracy on everything. It means Articos surfaces the themes human experts found. It doesn't mean it matches on emotional nuance, on behavioral edge cases, or on discovering what you didn't know to ask. Per-domain scores vary across the 9 tested domains. The right use case is directional triage: validate the concept before you invest in recruitment, test the message before you run the full study. Discovery research still needs real humans.

The bottleneck that moved: not "can I afford research?" That's now 30 minutes at $29. The new bottleneck is "do I have the right research question?" And "which themes from this synthetic round do I need to validate with real users?"

Your study design skill just became more valuable than your moderation skill.
