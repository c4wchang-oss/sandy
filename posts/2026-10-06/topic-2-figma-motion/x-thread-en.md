**Tweet 1 — Hook**
Design systems had tokens for color.
Tokens for typography.
Tokens for spacing.
Tokens for components.

Motion was always the orphan.
Figma Motion just changed that.

---

**Tweet 2**
Custom animation styles are now live in Figma Motion (open beta).

Save an easing curve + duration + variable-backed properties as a named style.
Publish it to your library.
Apply it across every file — exactly like a color style.

Motion is now a design system citizen.

---

**Tweet 3**
Why this matters for AI workflows specifically:

Every time someone describes animation to an AI tool, they say "smooth," "bouncy," or "fast."
Those words produce completely different results depending on the model.

A named style gives the brief a precision that language doesn't.

---

**Tweet 4**
MCP + Skills integration means coding agents can apply your motion styles programmatically.

The easing and timing values are readable by Figma agent and coding agents via MCP.

Brief quality for animation is now partially systematic, not just taste-dependent.

---

**Tweet 5**
Audio on the timeline is the update I didn't expect:

Drag any audio file onto a clip.
Trim, split, reposition against keyframes.
Export with MP4 or WebM.

Sound design now lives in the same layer as motion design.
That's a different kind of brief quality shift.

---

**Tweet 6**
Lottie / dotLottie export closes the design-to-production pipeline for web animation.

Dev Mode can inspect the full motion timeline and copy animation code as CSS, JSON, or React.

The handoff model for motion is now the same as for components.

---

**Tweet 7**
Honest caveats:
- Open beta — features may still change
- Manually animating never consumes AI credits; agent-applied motion does
- Quality ceiling = your motion style library (garbage in, garbage out)
- Character/word/line text animation is powerful and also the easiest way to over-animate a UI

The governance question: who owns and curates the motion style library?
Nobody has answered this yet.

---

**Tweet 8 — CTA**
For the past decade, every design system had an undocumented motion layer.

Easing curves lived in someone's Notion notes.
Duration values were in the Slack thread from 2022.
Nobody could apply them programmatically.

That era just ended.

The motion DesignOps conversation starts now.
