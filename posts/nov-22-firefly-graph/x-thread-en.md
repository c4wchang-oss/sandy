## THREADS POST

**[Long-form reflection]**

Three of the largest creative platforms converged on the same interface in 15 days.

Runway App Studio (Oct 27): describe a creative app, it builds and deploys it. The workflow becomes a tool anyone can run.

Figma Weave nodes (Oct 30): the node graph inside the design canvas IS the institutional knowledge — not a document about the process, but the process itself, traceable on the canvas.

Adobe Firefly Graph (Nov 10): node-based workflow canvas for connecting AI models, Creative Cloud tools, and assets into repeatable pipelines. Build once, run repeatedly, share with the team.

The pattern is the same across all three: the domain expert encodes their process as a runnable system, then steps back from being the bottleneck. Workflow authorship — not prompt authorship — is the actual skill.

Firefly Graph adds two things worth noting beyond the basics. First, subgraphs: group related nodes, expose only the inputs/outputs you want to surface, hide the internal steps. That's constraint architecture at the workflow layer. The person running the workflow can't accidentally break what's inside the subgraph. Second, model comparison: run multiple AI models with the same inputs and compare outputs side by side. That's AI quality governance as a built-in workflow step, not a separate process.

Then there's Firefly Builder. Natural language → interactive prototype, with a design system generated from your brand guidelines, direct on-canvas editing, and HTML export for development. The headline writes itself.

Except it's in private beta on a waitlist.

The most interesting capability is the least accessible. That's not a complaint — it's the honest state of things. What Builder confirms is that on-brand constraint infrastructure (design system baked in before generation, not bolted on after) is now the expected standard pattern for AI-assisted prototyping. That's the signal worth noting even if you can't use Builder today.

Honest caveat on Firefly Graph: public beta means feature changes expected. The AI Assistant for building workflows sounds useful — but "AI builds your creative process" means your institutional knowledge quality determines your output quality. The same quality ceiling problem as every other AI tool, now at the workflow architecture layer.
