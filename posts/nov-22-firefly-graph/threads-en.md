## THREADS POST — Long-form

Three of the largest creative platforms converged on the same interface type in 15 days. That's not coincidence — that's a category crystallizing.

Runway App Studio: Oct 27. Encode expert workflow → deployable app anyone can run.
Figma Weave nodes: Oct 30. Node graph inside design canvas = institutional knowledge that runs.
Adobe Firefly Graph: Nov 10. Node-based workflow canvas connecting AI models, CC tools, assets into repeatable pipelines.

All three are betting on workflow authorship. The domain expert's job is to encode their process into a runnable system, then step back from being the bottleneck. The node graph IS the documentation — except it runs.

What Firefly Graph does that the others don't: model comparison. Same inputs, multiple AI models, side-by-side outputs. That's AI quality governance as a built-in workflow step. Worth using just for that before anything else.

Subgraphs are the other thing worth learning first: group nodes, expose only what you want surfaced, hide internal complexity. Constraint architecture at the workflow layer. The person running your workflow gets "input brand assets → get campaign output" — they can't accidentally modify the intelligence inside.

Then there's Firefly Builder. Natural language → on-brand interactive prototype. Brand guidelines → design system. Build website/app/presentation. Export HTML. The headline writes itself.

Except it's in private beta on a waitlist.

This is the honest tension with Adobe MAX: they announce the genuinely exciting things in private beta or coming soon, and the things you can use today are more incremental. Firefly Graph is real and usable. Firefly Builder is a direction signal.

The direction it confirms: on-brand constraint infrastructure baked in before generation (not patched in after) is now the expected standard for AI-assisted prototyping. The model doesn't generate generic output and then you try to make it on-brand. The design system is the first input, not the last correction.

That's what all three workflow canvas platforms are building toward, in their own ways.
